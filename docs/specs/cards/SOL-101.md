# SOL-101 — Change a category's kind and carry its entries along

Owner request (2026-11-12): categories created as Diário that are really Saídas (Aluguel, Fies, Porto) can only be fixed today by recreating them and editing every entry.

Reworked to D27 (mixed groups, SOL-105): a category changes kind on its own; its subcategories keep theirs.

## Rules

- `PATCH /api/categories/{id}` accepts `kind`, `daily` or `bill` only.
- The change applies to one category, top-level or subcategory. Its subcategories keep their kind; `kind-of-subcategory` no longer exists.
- Economia, its cofrinhos, Resgate and income categories never change kind. The API refuses them with 422 `kind-locked`.
- Every entry of that category takes the new kind, series members included, all in one transaction.
- The new kind must not already hold an active category with the category's own name. A clash gives 409 `duplicate-name`, naming it.
- A category used by an unarchived conta fixa (active or paused) cannot become Diário. The API refuses it with 422 `category-has-bills`. Only contas fixas on that category count, not on its subcategories.
- A conta fixa is paid or resumed only while its category is Saída; otherwise the API refuses it with 422 on `category_id`.
- A top-level keeps its color when it is free in every kind the group then shows in, else takes the lowest free one and its subcategories wear it; with none free the change is refused with 422 `palette-full`.
- A subcategory keeps wearing its parent's color; the parent applies D26 (moves to a color free in every kind it shows in, or keeps its own when none is).
- The running balance does not change: Diário and Saída are both outflow.
- `kind` with other fields in one PATCH: the kind changes first, then the rest applies under the same rules as alone. A Diário top-level sent `{kind: "bill", parent_id: <Economia>}` becomes Saída, then moves under Economia as a cofrinho wearing Economia's color.
- An entry written while its category changes kind waits for the change and is checked against the final kind.

## Web

- In Ajustes › Categorias › Editar, a Diário or Saída category, top-level or subcategory, shows `Tipo` with `Diário` and `Saída`.
- Changing it asks for confirmation: `Mudar para {Saída|Diário}?` with `Todos os lançamentos de {nome} passam a ser {Saída|Diário}. O saldo não muda.` and `Mudar tipo` / `Cancelar`.
- Errors use the problem's detail, in pt-BR.
