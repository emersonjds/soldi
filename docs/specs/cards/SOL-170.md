# SOL-170: Planilha uses the full width on wide screens

## Problem

On a wide monitor (owner's screenshot, viewport ~2500px) `/mes` with the Planilha open sits inside the shell's `max-w-6xl` (`web/src/widgets/app-shell/app-shell.tsx`, `<main>`), so large empty bands frame the page. Inside it the Planilha cuts the third month: its breakpoints (`planilha.tsx`, `MonthBlock`: 1 month, 2 at `@[40rem]`, 3 at `@[64rem]`) give each month `(100cqi - gaps) / n`, but a month with real balances (`10.025,52`, 5 columns at 14px) needs more than that, so the tables grow past their `min-w` and the last one is cut, with horizontal scroll.

## Decision (owner approved, 2026-11-24)

1. **Page width.** With the Planilha open, the shell `<main>` drops its `max-w-6xl` cap and uses the available width. Every other screen, and `/mes` in Lista mode, keeps `max-w-6xl`. Done in CSS with `:has()` (the Planilha root carries a `data-planilha` attribute; `<main>` gets `has-[[data-planilha]]:max-w-none`), no state lifted into the shell.
2. **Whole months only.** The Planilha never shows a partially visible month at rest (snap position). Months are added as the container grows instead of stretching columns: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 months. Each breakpoint is chosen so every month gets at least the width its widest realistic row needs: balances and amounts up to `123.456,78` (6 integer digits, two thousand groups is out of scope) at the current 14px, with the current cell padding. Breakpoints are relative (`@[Nrem]` container queries, `cqi`), never fixed px widths.
3. **Cap at 5 months.** The approved proposal said "4 months above ~96rem instead of stretching columns"; at 2560px the uncapped container is ~139rem, where 4 months would stretch each to ~35rem. A fifth breakpoint keeps the same rule (no stretching) on the widest monitors. Above the fifth breakpoint the months stretch.
4. **No font change, no column change.** Font stays `clamp(0.75rem, 3.6cqi, 0.875rem)`; columns stay Data, Entrada, Saída, Diário, Saldo. Cell horizontal padding may drop from `px-2` to `px-1.5` only if that is what keeps 3 months at 1440px; otherwise 1440 shows 2 whole months and the e2e expectation changes accordingly.

## Measured (Chromium, 14px)

- A month with a `123.456,78` income and balance: 370px at `px-2`, 350px (21.9rem) at `px-1.5`. 3 months at `px-2` need 72rem; the scroller at 1440 is 69rem, so the padding drops to `px-1.5`.
- Breakpoints (n × 21.9rem + (n − 1) × 1rem, rounded up): 2 at `@[45rem]`, 3 at `@[68rem]`, 4 at `@[91rem]`, 5 at `@[114rem]`.
- Scroller = viewport − 21rem on desktop: 1024 → 1 month, 1280 → 2, 1440 → 3, 1920 → 4, 2560 → 5.
- With Saída and Diário also at `123.456,78` a month is 411px; such a month grows past its `min-w` at these breakpoints.

## Acceptance criteria

- Planilha open, at 375, 1024, 1280, 1440, 1920 and 2560 wide (height 900): no horizontal page scroll, `expectNoClipped` passes, and the number of whole months in view equals the breakpoint count; no month is partially visible inside the scroller.
- Holds with wide values: the e2e creates an entry that makes a day's balance reach 6 integer digits (e.g. an income of 123.456,78) before measuring.
- At 1920 and 2560 the Planilha card is wider than at 1440 (cap removed).
- Lista mode at 1920: `<main>` is still capped at `max-w-6xl` (width equal to 72rem).
- Switching Planilha → Lista restores the cap without reload.
- Loading stays lazy: on first load the fetched months are those in view plus one neighbour on each side.
