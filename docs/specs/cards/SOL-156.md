# SOL-156 — Lançar a Conta: "Conta fixa" and "Parcelado" switches

Owner 2026-11-22: "Recorrente" reads like a Spotify subscription on a card. Having both a Conta series and a conta fixa parcelada is more than he wants to think about. He picked this design: on a Conta, two mutually exclusive switches, and no "Criar como Conta Fixa" link.

## Scope

It applies only where today's "Recorrente" switch shows (`canRepeat`: a new entry, or an edited standalone entry) and only when the kind is Conta (`bill`).

- **Entrada and Diário keep "Recorrente" exactly as today**: salário Sempre, or a subscription on a card.
- **Editing a series member does not change.** "Editar Lançamento Recorrente" keeps Quantas vezes, Ciclo Atual and the SOL-152 "Criar como Conta Fixa" link.

## The two switches (Conta)

Each is a row like today's Recorrente, at least 44 px tall, with a muted background and the label on the left.

1. **"Conta fixa"** with the `Repeat` icon. While on, a caption below reads "Todo mês, sem fim. Ao salvar, abre o cadastro da conta fixa."
   - Salvar does what SOL-149's Sempre does today: it opens Nova conta prefilled from the entry, and saving that creates the entry and the conta fixa. When editing a stored standalone entry, it converts that entry, as `convert` does today.
   - The switch shows only where the host offers a conta fixa sheet (`onConvert` present). Without one, as when editing a card entry from the fatura, only Parcelado shows.
2. **"Parcelado"** with the `Layers` icon from lucide-react, already installed. While on, it shows:
   - Frequência (Mensal / Semanal / Anual), as today. A weekly diarista stays reachable.
   - The label "Quantas parcelas" (not "Quantas vezes") with presets 2, 3, 6, 12 and Outro. There is no "Sempre".
   - No preset starts selected. Salvar with none chosen shows "Escolha quantas parcelas." and does not save.
   - "Já começou em outra parcela?", the summary line ("N× de R$ … · …") and the 29–31 note stay as today.

Turning one switch on turns the other off. Turning a switch off clears its choices, as today's Recorrente does.

## Removed from Lançar

- The "Prefere transformar em conta fixa recorrente? Criar como Conta Fixa" link on a new or standalone Conta. The Conta fixa switch replaces it. It stays on a series member (SOL-152).
- The alert "Uma conta sem fim é uma conta fixa. Escolha quantas vezes." can no longer appear on a Conta. Remove the dead branch if nothing else reaches it.

## Acceptance

- A1: on a Conta, Lançar shows the "Conta fixa" and "Parcelado" switches and no "Recorrente" and no "Criar como Conta Fixa" link. On Entrada and Diário it shows "Recorrente" as today.
- A2: Conta fixa + Salvar opens Nova conta prefilled, and saving creates the linked entry and the conta fixa ("Conta fixa criada").
- A3: Parcelado + 3 + Salvar creates 3 entries ("3 lançamentos criados"). Weekly works. Salvar with no count shows "Escolha quantas parcelas.".
- A4: the switches are exclusive, and switching kind Conta → Diário shows Recorrente off.
- A5: without a conta fixa host, only Parcelado shows on a Conta.
- A6: a series member's edit sheet is unchanged (Quantas vezes, Ciclo Atual, Criar como Conta Fixa).
- A7: 375 to 1440 with no clipping and no sideways scroll. Touch targets are at least 44 px.
