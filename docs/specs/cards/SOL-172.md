# SOL-172: today's row flashes after "Ir pra hoje"

## Decision (owner, 2026-11-24)

In Modo planilha on `/mes`, "Ir pra hoje" scrolls to today but the row is hard to spot. After the jump, today's row flashes; no always-on tint. Keyboard focus, the today badge on Data and the RedDayAlert ring stay as they are. Nothing changes outside planilha mode.

## Behavior

- "Ir pra hoje" gives today's row `data-flash` for 2400 ms, cleared by a timeout that is cleaned up on unmount. Clicking again restarts the timeout and the animation.
- The flash is an overlay over the whole row, above the cells: a positioned `::after`, `inset-0`, `pointer-events-none`, background `--ring` at 18% (`bg-ring/18`, a `color-mix` in oklab). It shows over the weekend tint, the tier-colored Saldo and the gridlines, and follows the picked palette (D52).
- Animation: two soft pulses over 2.4 s, opacity 0 → 1 → 0.3 → 1 → 0, as `@keyframes today-flash` behind the `animate-today-flash` utility in `web/src/app/globals.css`.

## Reduced motion

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: no animation; the overlay stays static at full flash opacity for the 2.4 s.

## Acceptance

- After "Ir pra hoje", today's row has `data-flash`; 2400 ms later it does not.
- A second click inside the window restarts the 2400 ms.
- No other day's row ever gets `data-flash`.
- e2e (`web/e2e/planilha.spec.ts`): today's row has `data-flash` right after the click; `expectNoClipped` unchanged.
