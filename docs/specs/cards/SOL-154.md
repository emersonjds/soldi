# SOL-154 — Lançamentos on Mês: the whole month at once, filters by kind of bill

Owner ask 2026-11-22: Lançamentos opens with the whole month, with no "Ver o mês inteiro" or "Mostrar menos", and the filters are Todos, Diário, Contas fixas and Fixas com valor variável. Today the filters are Todos, Diário and Contas.

Decided by the orchestrator. The owner named the filters, and the rest follows existing rules (D44 `variable`, SOL-153 groups).

## What stays exactly as today

- The per-day header: "<dia>", then "Projeção:" (or "Saldo ao final:"), the money and the Folga / Apertado / Confortável pill. Under a filter it still shows the whole day's projection, never a filtered sum.
- Rows, their order (newest day first), the row sheet (Editar / Excluir, Desfazer for 5 s), "Motivo da queda", the ringed day after a jump from the hero, and the future-day opacity.

## Changes

1. **Whole month on open.** The §3.3 cap, the `expanded` state and the "Ver o mês inteiro" / "Mostrar menos" button go away, and every day with rows renders. A jump from the hero only resets the filter to Todos and scrolls. A month holds a few hundred rows at most, so the list renders without virtualization (ponytail: virtualize when a real account passes ~1,000 rows a month).
2. **Filters, in this order:** `Todos` · `Diário` · `Contas fixas` · `Valor variável`.
   - Todos: every row, as today.
   - Diário: entries of kind `daily` that are not money put in a cofrinho (today's rule).
   - Contas fixas: entries of kind `bill`, not saved, and not the payment of a variable conta fixa, plus unpaid due rows (`DueRow`) of non-variable bills. A Conta series that was never converted counts here, because the owner sees every monthly Conta as a conta fixa.
   - Valor variável: entries whose `recurring_bill_id` is a bill with `variable: true`, plus unpaid due rows with `variable: true`. The web learns which bills are variable from the bills query it already has (`useBills({ includeInactive: true })`, as SOL-153 does).
   - Under a filter, a day with no matching rows is hidden, as today.
3. **Empty texts:** Diário "Nenhum gasto diário neste mês." (as today), Contas fixas "Nenhuma conta fixa neste mês.", Valor variável "Nenhuma conta de valor variável neste mês.".
4. **Layout of the filter at 375:** four segments with full labels do not fit in 343 px. Below `lg` the toggle group is a 2 × 2 grid with equal columns, each segment at least 44 px tall and 16 px text. From `lg` up it is one row, as today. There is no horizontal scroll and no clipped label at 375, 1024, 1280 or 1440.

## Acceptance

- A1: on open, every row of the month is in the DOM and no "Ver o mês inteiro" / "Mostrar menos" control exists.
- A2: each filter shows only its kind, following §2, including due rows. A variable bill's payment and its unpaid due row appear under Valor variável and never under Contas fixas.
- A3: a day header shows the same projection, money and pill under every filter.
- A4: each filter's empty text shows when it has no rows.
- A5: the hero jump still rings and scrolls to its day, resetting the filter to Todos.
- A6: from 375 to 1440, with R$ 99.999,99 and long names, nothing clips and the page never scrolls sideways.
