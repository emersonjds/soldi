# SOL-119 — Card panel: what weighs on the fatura, and what is already committed ahead

Owner (2026-11-12): "pagamentos no cartão podem ter a opção de recorrente, sem data fim; eu preciso ver o painel e entender que no cartão estou gastando tanto e ver o que está me estrangulando, para parar de usar ou apagar a inscrição. […] minhas contas, meus limites, ter uma projeção de próximos meses se eu quiser comprar algo."

Already shipped in SOL-118 (0.58.0): card subscriptions (`open_ended` + `card_id`, D36 update), `Parar recorrência` / `Tornar sem fim` on a member, `k/N` and `Todo mês` chips. This card adds only **the panel**.

## Panel outcome (one pass: product, ux, finance; architect arbitrates)

### 1. Where it lives

| Vote | Option |
|---|---|
| product | A new `Cartões` screen listing every card with its fatura. |
| ux | Tap the `Fatura Porto` row in Contas a pagar; a sheet opens. Same reach at 375 and 1440, no new route. |
| finance | Either way, as long as the month is the fatura's due month, the same one Contas a pagar shows. |

**Winner: ux.** The fatura row in `Contas a pagar` (`widgets/bills-of-month`) gets a tappable name area (icon, `Fatura {nome}`, chevron) that opens a `ResponsiveSheet` titled `Fatura {nome}`. The round check, the amount (Pagar) and `BillStatus` keep their jobs. The sheet opens for the row's own month (`due_date`'s month), so a fatura carried from October (D17) opens October's.

Trade-off: a card month with no spends and no payment is not listed (D28), so its panel can't be reached that month. That is D25: there is nothing to show. Next months are one swipe away on the month switcher. Rejected: a `Cartões` route, which duplicates Contas a pagar and adds navigation to a product that must stay simple. Also rejected: a second entry point from Contas fixas, which is a settings screen (YAGNI; add it when asked).

### 2. What it shows (375 first, top to bottom)

1. **Total**: the fatura total (`total`, D30a carry included, the same number Contas a pagar shows unpaid), large, `Money`.
2. **State line** under it:
   - unpaid, due ahead: `Vence dia 10/12`;
   - unpaid, past due: `Venceu dia 10/11` in `text-expense` (D17);
   - paid in full (`paid ≥ total`): `Paga`;
   - partial (D30a, `0 < paid < total`): `Pagou R$ 300,00 · R$ 150,00 vão para a fatura de dezembro`.
3. **Carry** (only when `carried > 0`): one plain line, `Restante da fatura de outubro` and the amount.
4. **What weighs on it**: up to three groups in a fixed order. The order is learnable and the recurring weights you can cut come first. An empty group does not render (D25).
   - `Assinaturas`: members of open series (`series.open_ended`). Caption `Todo mês · R$ 478,80 por ano` (monthly ×12), `Toda semana · R$ 1.300,00 por ano` (×52), or `Todo ano`.
   - `Parcelas`: members of bounded series. Caption `3/10 · faltam 7`; the last one reads `10/10 · última`.
   - `Compras`: entries outside a series. Caption is the purchase day, `12 out`.
   - Group header: name on the left, `R$ 120,00 · 25%` on the right (the share of `total`, whole percent, `wholeShare`).
   - Rows: category icon, title (`note`, else the category name, truncated), caption, amount. Largest first, ties by title.
   - Several members of one series in one fatura (weekly) are **one row**: the amount is their sum, the caption gains `· 4 nesta fatura`, and the tap opens the latest member.
5. **Próximos meses**: the next 6 faturas, `jan/27 … R$ 380,00`, zero months included. Caption: `Já comprometido: assinaturas, parcelas e compras feitas.` A `Posso comprar?` link goes to `/posso-comprar` (existing simulator). The section hides when all six are zero (D25).

Votes on 4–5:

- finance wanted the yearly cost on subscriptions ("R$ 39,90 looks small; R$ 478,80 a year is what makes you cancel"). Accepted: one multiplication, web only.
- product wanted 12 months ahead. Cut to 6: it fits 375 without a scroll inside the sheet, and the 12-month view already exists in Mês › Próximos meses.
- ux wanted a bar per month. Cut: a list of six amounts reads at a glance with no new component (`pixel` stays out).

### 3. Actions

| Vote | Option |
|---|---|
| ux | An inline `Parar` button on each subscription row. |
| architect | A row tap opens the existing `TransactionSheet` for that entry. |

**Winner: architect.** The sheet already holds `Parar recorrência` (toast + `Desfazer`), `Tornar sem fim` and every edit with its D30b `fatura-paid` refusal. It opens **over** the panel (nested Radix dialog, the same pattern as `billSheet` over Lançar). The panel refetches when the sheet's mutation invalidates `["month"]`. Trade-off: one more tap to cancel a subscription; zero duplicated mutation, toast or undo logic.

`Excluir` is not offered from the panel (`onDelete` not passed). "Apagar a inscrição" is `Parar recorrência`: the subscription leaves every following fatura, and past and paid ones stay (D35). Deleting history stays in Lançamentos.

### 4. Limits

| Vote | Position |
|---|---|
| product | In: owner said "meus limites". |
| finance | Out: a card limit is consumed by the **whole** value of a parcelado purchase and never by future subscription charges. Soldi's rows are the opposite: parcelas are monthly rows, and subscriptions have rows up to 12 months ahead (D36). A "Limite disponível" from rows would be wrong both ways; D16 says never guess. |

**Winner: finance. Out of SOL-119**, follow-up card `Limite do cartão` (optional `credit_limit` on a card bill, usage = unpaid spends of open and closed faturas + remaining parcelas at full value, subscription rows excluded). Its own panel decides the usage rule.

### 5. Data: one read endpoint (architect)

Web-only option counted: fatura `m` holds spends from months `m−2..m` (closing and due days decide). Six faturas ahead need months `m−2..m+6`. That is up to **9** `GET /api/transactions?month=` calls, each returning every entry of the month and each running D36 growth. The D30a carry can't be derived at all without every payment since the opening. Rejected.

**Endpoint: `GET /api/months/{month}/faturas/{card_id}`, 1 request.** It sits beside `GET /api/months/{month}` in the months slice, where `faturas()` already lives.

## Technical design (architect)

### Contract (`crates/soldi-types/src/months.rs`)

```rust
/// SOL-119: a card's fatura due in `month`, what fills it, and the next six.
pub struct CardFatura {
    pub month: String,               // YYYY-MM
    pub total: Decimal,              // spends + D30a carry: the number Contas a pagar shows unpaid
    pub carried: Decimal,            // left from last month's partial payment (D30a), else 0
    pub paid: Decimal,               // the card's payments dated in `month`, else 0
    pub entries: Vec<Transaction>,   // the fatura's spends, newest date first, with `series`
    pub ahead: Vec<FaturaAhead>,     // months m+1..m+6, zero included
}
pub struct FaturaAhead { pub month: String, pub total: Decimal }
```

Money serializes as a string (`serialize_with = "crate::money"`), as in `BillOfMonth`.

Errors:

- 401 without a session;
- 404 when `card_id` is not one of the caller's cards (another user's card, a plain bill, an unknown id);
- 400 when `card_id` is not a UUID;
- 422 `validation` / `month-before-opening`, as `GET /api/months/{month}`.

### Read path (`months::service::fatura`): D36 growth, then 5 queries

1. `transactions::service::extend_open_series(open_until(today, month_end(m+6)))`: grows subscriptions so the ahead totals are committed rows (D16, D36).
2. `settings::repo::find`: the opening date; `check_opening` (extracted from `get`) refuses an earlier month.
3. `months::repo::bills`: archived cards included (D28). The card is the row with this id and a `closing_day`, else 404. This is the user scope: `bills` filters by `user_id`.
4. `months::repo::day_sums(opening, month_end(m+6), cards_from(opening))`: the existing `GROUP BY date` query.
5. `months::repo::payments(first_of(opening), month_end(m+6))`.
6. `transactions::repo::list(user, m−2 months, month_end(m), None, Some(card_id))`: gains `card_id: Option<Uuid>` (`AND ($5::uuid IS NULL OR t.card_id = $5)`), still `WHERE t.user_id = $1`.

Pure `months::domain::card_fatura(card, closing_day, month, &faturas(..), &payments, entries)`:

- keeps the entries whose `fatura_due` falls in `month`;
- `total` comes from the shared `faturas()` map (D30a carry included);
- `carried` = last month's `max(0, total − paid)` when last month had a payment, else 0. This is D30a exactly as `faturas()` carries it; an unpaid month stays its own D17 row;
- `paid` = the card's payments in `month`;
- `ahead` = `faturas` for m+1..m+6, `0` when absent.

Same numbers as Mês, one code path.

### Web

- `entities/bill/fatura-query.ts`: `faturaQuery(month, cardId)`, key `["month", month, "fatura", cardId]`, so every bill and transaction mutation's existing `["month"]` invalidation refreshes it.
- `entities/bill/fatura-groups.ts`: pure `faturaGroups(fatura)` → groups and rows (cents math via `cents`/`fromCents`, share via `wholeShare`, chips via `OPEN_LABEL`).
- `features/card-fatura/fatura-sheet.tsx`: `FaturaSheet` (`ResponsiveSheet`), with render prop `entrySheet` for the entry editor (FSD: a feature never imports a feature).
- `widgets/bills-of-month/bills-of-month.tsx`: the card row's name area opens `FaturaSheet`; the widget passes `TransactionSheet` (as `widgets/ledger` does).

## States and acceptance criteria

- **Entry**: a card row in Contas a pagar shows a chevron after `Fatura {nome}`. Tapping the name area (≥ 44 px) opens `Fatura {nome}`. A non-card row has no such button. The round check, the amount and the status button behave as before.
- **Mixed fatura** (Spotify open 39,90; notebook 3/10 of 250,00; two compras 80,00 and 30,00): total 399,90. Groups in the order Assinaturas (39,90 · 10%), Parcelas (250,00 · 63%), Compras (110,00 · 28%), rows largest first. Spotify reads `Todo mês · R$ 478,80 por ano`; the notebook reads `3/10 · faltam 7`.
- **Only subscriptions**: only `Assinaturas` renders, at 100%.
- **Weekly subscription, 4 members in the fatura**: one row, the 4 summed, `· 4 nesta fatura`.
- **Last parcela**: `10/10 · última`; the ahead list drops by its amount the next month.
- **No spends this month**: the row is not listed (D28), so there is no entry point. If reached with only a carry, the panel shows total = carry, the carry line, no groups.
- **Paid fatura**: state `Paga`. Rows open the entry sheet; editing a spend's amount, date or card there is refused with `Essa fatura já foi paga.` (D30b, existing). `Parar recorrência` on a paid fatura's member works and only later, unpaid faturas lose it (D35, SOL-118 test).
- **Partial payment** (450 paid 300): `Pagou R$ 300,00 · R$ 150,00 vão para a fatura de {próximo}`. The next month's panel shows `Restante da fatura de {este}` R$ 150,00 inside its total.
- **Archived card with an unpaid fatura**: listed (D28), panel identical.
- **Parar recorrência from the panel**: the entry sheet opens over the panel. After the toast, the panel's Próximos meses drops by the subscription in every later month; this month keeps it.
- **Ahead all zero**: `Próximos meses` does not render.
- **Error**: the body shows `Não foi possível carregar.` + `Tentar de novo`; the title stays.
- **Loading**: title only, no skeleton (D25).
- **User isolation**: another user's card id → 404, and nothing of theirs is ever listed.
- **375 px**: bottom sheet, no horizontal scroll, titles truncate, amounts `whitespace-nowrap`, every tap target ≥ 44 px. 1440: right sheet, same content.
