# SOL-169: the simulator weighs the month's Diário previsto, not one day's

- Cause: `outflowsOf` (`web/src/widgets/purchase-simulator/results.tsx`) ranked contas summed over the red month up to the red day against the red day's previsto alone, so "Diário previsto" almost never made the two named causes.
- Rule (D49 context): like with like. The previsto weighs as the sum of the per-day `previsto` in the month's `MonthView.days` up to the red day, the same days the contas land on. No settings recompute, no API change; label stays "Diário previsto".
- A1: 20 days × R$ 40,00 of previsto (R$ 800,00) against a R$ 500,00 conta names "Diário previsto" first.
- A2: a conta larger than the month's previsto stays first.
- A3: a month with no previsto keeps the contas' ranking unchanged.
