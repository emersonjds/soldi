# SOL-104 — Economia do ano shows Resgatado next to Guardado

Owner request (2026-11-12). Today a month whose resgates passed its deposits shows a negative Guardado (Maio −R$ 3.851,83). A resgate should read as money taken back out, not as negative saving.

## Rules

- `SavingsMonth` gains `deposited` and `withdrawn`, both Decimal strings ≥ 0. `deposited` is the month's Saídas under Economia (itself or a cofrinho). `withdrawn` is the month's resgates. `saved` stays `deposited − withdrawn`, and `rate_pct` stays `saved / income`.
- `SavingsYear` gains `deposited_total` and `withdrawn_total`, over the months up to today's (forecast months excluded, as with `saved_total`).
- The totals come from the same `GROUP BY` month query, with no new query.

## Web (Economia do ano)

- Table (≥ 1024): `GUARDADO` shows `deposited`; a new column `RESGATADO` after it shows `withdrawn` as `−R$ x` in `--expense-text`, or `—` at zero. `% GUARDADO` keeps the net rate.
- List (< 1024): the amount shows `deposited`; when `withdrawn > 0`, a second line reads `Resgatado R$ x`.
- The summary `TOTAL CONSOLIDADO` stays the net `saved_total`. When `withdrawn_total > 0`, a line under it reads `Guardado R$ {deposited_total} · Resgatado R$ {withdrawn_total}`.
- CSV: columns `Mês;Entradas;Guardado;Resgatado;% Guardado;Situação`.
- Consistência and Meses no Azul keep using net `saved > 0`.
