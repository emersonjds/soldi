# SOL-148 — Subcategories fold on Para onde vai at mobile widths

Owner ask 2026-11-15: on Para onde vai the desktop unfolds a group into its subcategories and amounts; the phone could not.

Decision: reuse the desktop chevron (44 px target, `aria-expanded`) on every group with subcategories, at every width. No eye icon: in Brazilian bank apps an eye toggles hiding balances, so it would read as privacy, not detail.

Acceptance:
- At 375 px a group with subcategories shows the chevron beside its row; a group without subcategories shows none.
- Tapping the chevron lists each subcategory with its amount, plus "<Group> (sem subcategoria)" when the group holds money of its own; tapping again folds it.
- Tapping the row itself still picks the group (Transações panel), as before.
- Below 1024 px the list indents 16 px instead of 56 px so long names fit; desktop is unchanged.
- "Expandir tudo" and the "N subcategorias" count stay desktop only; the chevron is the cue on the phone.
- Nothing clips from 375 to 1440 (`web/e2e/reports.spec.ts`).
