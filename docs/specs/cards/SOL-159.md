# SOL-159 — Lançamentos: a conta reads as a conta, paid or not

Owner, production 0.66.0, /mes, 2026-11-23. Contas a pagar lists Parcela Carro (paid, dia 12), Celular (dia 26) and Gás (dia 30). In Lançamentos the paid Parcela Carro reads "Veículo › Parcela Carro · Saída", and the unpaid Celular reads "Conta fixa · a pagar". Owner model (2026-11-23, closed): a Conta is a **Conta fixa** (every month, no end) or a **Conta parcelada** (N months). Both are `recurring_bills`, and in Lançamentos a conta always reads as a conta, never as a loose "Saída".

Web only: captions in `web/src/widgets/ledger/ledger.tsx`. No contract change, no new component, no flow change. One-agent panel: ux decides, with product and finance lenses.

## What stays exactly as today

- Row title: the entry's note, or else its category path ("Veículo › Parcela Carro"). Icon, money, "Motivo da queda", the row sheet (Editar / Excluir, Desfazer for 5 s), day headers, order, and the ring after a jump from the hero.
- The filters: same four segments, same labels, same order, same contents, same empty texts, and the same 2 × 2 grid below `lg` (SOL-154). Choosing a filter still takes 1 tap.
- A loose Saída (no `recurring_bill_id`) still reads "Saída". "Guardado" and "Resgatado" are unchanged. The series chip "↻ Saída · 1/2" is unchanged (series conversion is SOL-160).
- DueRow keeps `aria-label` = bill name (plus " de <mês>" when carried from an earlier month).

## Changes

1. **Paid conta caption.** A `bill` entry with `recurring_bill_id` that is not money saved gets its caption from the bill. The source, in this order:
   - the month's `BillOfMonth` whose `transaction_id` equals the entry id. A parcelada gives "Parcela X/N · paga", and its last parcela gives "Última parcela · paga" (the same words as Contas a pagar, SOL-153). A fixa gives "Conta fixa · paga". A card gives "Fatura · paga".
   - otherwise, the `Bill` with that id from `useBills({ includeInactive: true })`. This covers a bill paid in another month's view. A bill with `installments` gives "Conta parcelada · paga", with no X/N: the payment date does not tell which parcela it paid when it was paid late (D35 counts a parcela in its own month). A card gives "Fatura · paga". Anything else gives "Conta fixa · paga".
   - while the bills are loading, or when the bill is not found: "Conta fixa · paga". This matches `filterOf`, which treats a payment as fixed until the bills load.
2. **Unpaid rows (`dueCaption`)** take the same first word: "Conta fixa", "Parcela X/N" ("Última parcela" on the last one) or "Fatura". The status words stay as today: "· a pagar", "· venceu dia 12/11", "· venceu em outubro". Examples: "Parcela 3/12 · a pagar", "Última parcela · venceu dia 12/11", "Fatura · venceu em outubro".
3. **Variable:** an unpaid row of a `variable` bill appends "· valor variável" (the SOL-152 rule: marked while unpaid). A paid variable conta is never marked; it reads "Conta fixa · paga".
4. The sheet description (`caption · label`) follows on its own, e.g. "Parcela 3/12 · paga · Veículo › Parcela Carro".
5. Wrapping: the Row caption line gets `flex-wrap`, so a long caption wraps instead of clipping at 375 px. DueRow's caption already wraps.
