# SOL-186 — A conta fixa pays itself on its due day

Owner, 2026-11-25 (binding). Registering a conta fixa already states amount and due day, so tapping Pagar every month repeats the same fact. Every conta fixa and conta parcelada with a fixed amount counts as paid on its due day with no tap; before the due day it stays "a pagar" in Projetado exactly as today. The paid row's action becomes "Não paguei"; an undone month stays open and red after the due day until paid (D17). Manual, as today: `valor variável` (D44) and cards/faturas (D28, D30). Paying early and paying another amount still work. Builds on D8, D13, D17, D35, D42, D44, D46, D54.

## 1. Current code

- Paying = one `transactions` row with `recurring_bill_id` (`bills::service::pay`); one a month by the unique index `transactions_bill_month_idx` (0008), mapped to 409 `bill-already-paid` by `transactions::service::paid_once`. Unpay = `DELETE /api/bills/{id}/pay?month=` (`transactions::repo::delete_payment`).
- `months::domain::unpaid` / `owed` decide what is owed (opening date, `active_since`, `runs_in` for D35 `last_month`/`installments`, card fatura > 0). Month view, Contas a pagar, D17 carry and forecast share them.
- Precedent for lazy writes on read: `transactions::service::extend_open_series` (D36) grows open series before 4 readers (month, fatura, forecast, Lista), truncating to the D42 ceiling under `repo::lock_entries`. Reports, cofrinhos and the quota count do not call it.
- `months::repo::bills` loads every unarchived bill + every card as `MonthBill` (has `amount`, `due_day`, `closing_day`, `active_since`, `last_month`, `installments`, `variable`).
- `bills::repo::update` writes `active_since = São Paulo today` when `restart` (Retomar, D46 drop of an ended plan).
- Web: `widgets/bills-of-month/bills-of-month.tsx` groups rows as `fixed | variable | card` (from `variable`, `is_card`); the round check toggles `usePayBill` / `useUnpayBill`; `useUnpayBill` already patches `overdue: due_date < today`.

## 2. Decisions

### 2.1 Materialize, never derive (Q1)

Rule: an auto-paid month is a real `transactions` row, the same row Pagar writes (`kind = bill`, `recurring_bill_id`, bill's amount and category, dated on the due date). It is inserted lazily, idempotently, once per user per São Paulo day, before any signed-in request is served.

Why: D8 keeps transactions the only ledger. Deriving a virtual payment means teaching every reader of the ledger about it:

| Reader | Derive would change |
| --- | --- |
| Month view running balance, tier, `Saldo hoje` (`months::repo::day_sums`) | synthetic `DaySum` per virtual payment |
| D17 carry, Contas a pagar, Projetado (`months::domain::unpaid`) | treat virtual as paid |
| 6/12-month forecast (`months::service::forecast`) | same |
| Gastos, Para onde vai (`reports::repo::category_sums`, D54) | UNION of virtual rows |
| Meu ano (`reports::service::year`) | same |
| Economia / Guardado (`months::repo::saved`, `reports::service::savings`) for a bill in Economia | same |
| Lista and Modo planilha (`transactions::service::list`) | virtual rows with no id: Editar/Excluir/"Editar conta" break |
| Bills list paid flags (`bills::repo::paid_in`, `paid_since`) | same |
| Entry quota D42 (`transactions::repo::count_period`) | a virtual row escapes the ceiling |

Nine readers plus every by-id write. Materialize changes **zero readers**: 1 migration, 1 domain fn, 4 repo fns, 1 service fn, 1 call in the `CurrentUser` extractor, 1 SQL tweak in `bills::repo::update`. Cofrinho/fatura freezes never meet it (cards are excluded).

Trigger site: the `CurrentUser` extractor (`crate::extract`), after the session lookup, calls `bills::service::auto_pay(&state.pool, &user, crate::today_sao_paulo())`. One call covers every reader, Meu ano and Para onde vai included, which the `extend_open_series` per-reader pattern would miss on a fresh day. Cost when nothing is due: one indexed `SELECT EXISTS` per request; the catch-up itself runs once per user per day.

Rejected: derive at read time (9 readers, ids missing, quota hole); a scheduled job (no scheduler in the app, a missed run leaves Venceu rows); catch-up in each reader like D36 (call sites drift; Meu ano opened first would miss the day).

### 2.2 The watermark: `recurring_bills.auto_paid_through` (Q2, Q3)

One `date NOT NULL` column: the last São Paulo day whose due occurrences were already auto-paid. The catch-up pays occurrences with `auto_paid_through < due_date ≤ today`, then moves the column to `today` for **every** bill of the user (variable, cards and paused ones included, so a later switch never backfills).

This one column settles history, Não paguei and Excluir with no skip table:

- **Não paguei** = the existing `DELETE /api/bills/{id}/pay?month=`. It deletes the row; the watermark is already past that due date, so nothing re-inserts it. The month is open: `overdue` after the due date, `Venceu em <mês>` carried (D17).
- **Pagar after Não paguei** = the existing `POST /api/bills/{id}/pay`, any amount and date in the month (D44 sheet or the round check). Manual from then on.
- **Excluir** of an auto-paid entry (Lista, entry sheet, `delete-entries` period wipe) = a plain delete, same effect as Não paguei. A wipe never resurrects payments.
- **Pay early**: the manual row holds the month; the catch-up's `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` skips it. Not paguei on an early payment before the due day: the month auto-pays on its due day (owner: before the due day it stays a pagar as today).
- **Another amount**: Não paguei then Pagar, or edit the entry (Editar in Lista). The auto row is a normal entry.

History rule (Q3): auto-pay starts the day the migration runs. Migration `0032_bill_auto_paid_through.sql` adds the column with `DEFAULT ((now() AT TIME ZONE 'America/Sao_Paulo')::date - 1)`: existing rows get deploy day − 1 (one evaluation, `now()` is stable inside the migration), new rows get registration day − 1. So:

- every occurrence due before the deploy day keeps today's state: paid stays paid, unpaid stays `Venceu` (D17). Nothing the user reconciled moves;
- an occurrence due on the deploy day or later auto-pays;
- a bill registered today with due day today auto-pays today (`active_since` = today, D17 already owes it).

Edits that start or restart owing set `auto_paid_through = LEAST(auto_paid_through, today − 1)` in `bills::repo::update`:

- `restart` (Retomar, D46 drop of an ended plan: `active_since` moves to today) — else a due-today month after Retomar would stay open and turn red tomorrow;
- `variable` true → false (old value in SQL `SET`) — today's due month auto-pays, earlier ones stay as they are.

Rejected: a `bill_skips (bill_id, month)` table for Não paguei (a second fact to keep in sync with three delete paths; the watermark already says it); auto-paying every unpaid month since `active_since` (rewrites balances and Gastos the user already reconciled, silently); a constant cutoff date in code (deploy day unknown when written; a slipped deploy would auto-pay unseen Venceu rows); a DB trigger writing skips on delete (hidden rule, fights cascade deletes); a `transactions.auto` provenance flag (§2.5).

### 2.3 Which occurrences auto-pay (Q4)

`months::domain::auto_paid(bill, after, opening, today) -> Vec<NaiveDate>`: the due dates `due_date(month, due_day)` of each month from `first_of(after)` to today's month with `after < due ≤ today` and `owed(bill, amount, due, opening)`, for a bill with `closing_day = None` and `!variable`. Reusing `owed` gives, unchanged:

| Rule | Effect on auto-pay |
| --- | --- |
| Opening date (D13) | no payment dated before it (the 422 `before-opening-date` can never trigger) |
| `active_since` / Retomar (D17) | nothing before the bill started owing; Retomar + §2.2 restart |
| Parar / `last_month` (D35) | nothing after the last month; `bill-ended` 422 can never trigger |
| Parcelada `installments` (D35, D46) | only months with parcela ≥ 1 |
| Archived bill | not in `months::repo::bills`, never paid |
| Category no longer `bill` kind | skipped in the INSERT's join (`c.kind = 'bill'`), like `pay`'s `check_bill_kind` |
| Due day 29–31 | clamped by `due_date`, as everywhere |
| Quota 50,000 (D42) | dates truncated to the room left, silently, like D36; the watermark still advances, so a cut month stays open and Pagar answers 422 `quota` |
| Multi-user | every statement filters `user_id = $1`; the FK `(user_id, recurring_bill_id)` already binds owner and bill |

Amount and category come from the bill row at catch-up time. Later edits of the bill never touch rows already written (ledger). Due-day edits leave the watermark: moving this month's due day to a day already past leaves that month open (`Venceu`) — tap Pagar (trade-off, owner may review).

Concurrency: `auto_pay` runs in one DB transaction that first takes the user's settings row (`settings::repo::find`, `FOR UPDATE`), then the user's entry lock (`transactions::repo::lock_entries`, the one `check_room` and `pay` take), then re-reads what is behind under it; nothing behind → commit at once, and the quota count runs only when something is due. Two requests at the day roll serialize; the second finds nothing behind. A concurrent manual Pagar loses to the unique index either way (409 `bill-already-paid` or `DO NOTHING`). Lock order is always settings → entries → bill rows, the same as `pay`, transaction create and cofrinhos (`check_opening`, then `check_room`, then the FK's `KEY SHARE`); entries before settings deadlocked a catch-up against a Pagar (`d55_a_catch_up_and_a_pay_never_deadlock`).

### 2.4 Money rules (Q5, finance)

| Figure | Before the due day | From the due day, auto-paid | After Não paguei |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Running balance / tier per day | unpaid lands on the due date (as today) | ledger row on the due date | lands on `max(due, today)` (D17) |
| `Saldo hoje` | unchanged | − amount from the due date | − amount (landing today), same value |
| `Projetado` (month end) | − amount | − amount | − amount |
| 6/12-month forecast | lands on due date | same day, same amount | lands today |
| Gastos (D54), Para onde vai, Meu ano | not counted | counted on the due date, in the bill's category | not counted |
| Guardado (bill in Economia) | not counted | counted | not counted |
| Entries quota | — | +1 entry | −1 entry |

Worked example, after the deploy (watermark live). 30/11 balance R$ 3.000,00. December: salário + R$ 5.000,00 on 05/12; Aluguel (fixa) R$ 1.500,00 due 10; Luz (valor variável) ≈ R$ 200,00 due 12, unpaid; Internet (fixa) R$ 120,00 due 28. Today 15/12.

| Day | Today's rule (tap needed, none tapped) | SOL-186 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 05–09/12 | 8.000,00 | 8.000,00 |
| 10–14/12 | 8.000,00 | 6.500,00 (Aluguel paid on 10/12) |
| 15/12 `Saldo hoje` | 8.000 − 1.500 (Venceu, today) − 200 (Luz Venceu) = 6.300,00 | 6.500 − 200 (Luz Venceu) = 6.300,00 |
| `Projetado` 31/12 | 6.300 − 120 = 6.180,00 | 6.180,00 |
| Gastos dezembro | R$ 0,00 from contas | R$ 1.500,00 (Aluguel) |
| 28/12 | Internet lands | Internet auto-paid, same day, same amount |

Não paguei on Aluguel on 15/12: the row is deleted, 10–14/12 go back to 8.000,00, `Saldo hoje` stays 6.300,00 (Aluguel lands today, Venceu), Gastos − 1.500,00. Pagar then R$ 1.550,00 dated 15/12: ledger − 1.550 on 15/12, `Saldo hoje` 6.250,00, Gastos 1.550,00.

Deploy-day history: deploy 26/11, watermark 25/11. November's Aluguel (due 10/11) unpaid stays `Venceu em novembro`, still − 1.500 landing today until tapped. Internet due 28/11 auto-pays on 28/11.

### 2.5 Contract (Q6)

No new endpoint, field or DTO.

- Não paguei = `DELETE /api/bills/{id}/pay?month=YYYY-MM` (exists). Pagar = `POST /api/bills/{id}/pay` (exists).
- "Pago automático" vs "pago manual" is not stored: both are the same ledger fact, nothing branches on it. The web already knows a row pays itself from `BillOfMonth.variable == false && !is_card` (the `fixed` group). The paid date the Caption already shows (`paidOn` vs `due_date`) tells "on the due day" if UX wants it.
- "Não paguei" is not a state: it is `paid: false`, `overdue` after the due date, `Venceu` carried (today's fields).
- Doc comments only: `soldi_types::bills::Bill.variable` and `soldi_types::months::BillOfMonth.paid` / `variable` gain "D55: a fixed-amount conta fixa is paid on its due day with no tap; a variable one stays manual". Refresh `web/openapi.json` (descriptions only; `pnpm gen:api` regenerates comments).

Rejected: `transactions.auto boolean` (migration + DTO for a label; a manual pay on the due day is the same fact); `POST /api/bills/{id}/skip` (Não paguei already exists as unpay); a `status` enum on the row (three booleans already encode it).

## 3. UX brief

Improve in place. What stays: the row layout, the round check (lg) and the status pill (mobile) as the only pay/undo control, one tap to pay early, one tap to undo, `Pagamento desfeito`, every caption and the Lista/planilha words. What changes: the fixed paid row's undo is named "Não paguei", its toast gains Desfazer, its amount opens the payment to fix the value, a desktop hint before the due day, one helper line in the form. No new component: `RoundCheck`, `BillStatus`/`PillButton`, `Caption`, `TransactionSheet` (already imported in `bills-of-month.tsx`), sonner `action` (same shape as `useArchiveLater`).

Market check (the step "conta fixa counts as paid by itself"): Mobills posts a fixed card expense by itself on its day ([Mobills help](https://mobills.zendesk.com/hc/pt-br/articles/4414666860059-Por-que-as-despesas-fixas-do-cart%C3%A3o-de-cr%C3%A9dito-n%C3%A3o-aparecem-nos-pr%C3%B3ximos-meses)); Organizze keeps recurring contas manual, a dashboard alert card with "marcar como pago" ([Organizze blog](https://www.organizze.com.br/blog/gestao-financeira/pagamentos-recorrentes)); YNAB scheduled transactions enter the register on their date and are edited or deleted like any entry. Soldi follows Mobills/YNAB for fixed contas and keeps Organizze's manual tap for `valor variável` and faturas.

## 4. Acceptance

1. `d55_a_fixed_conta_pays_on_each_due_day_after_the_watermark`: watermark 2026-09-20, today 2026-11-25, due 10 → `[2026-10-10, 2026-11-10]`; due 20 → `[2026-10-20, 2026-11-20]`; due 25 → includes 2026-11-25; due 26 → excludes November.

3. `d55_auto_pay_keeps_the_owed_gates`: before opening, before `active_since`, after `last_month`, parcela 0 → excluded; due day 31 in February → 28/02 (or 29 in a leap year).

Server integration (`crates/soldi-server/tests/bills.rs`, beside `rule_5_unpaying_deletes_the_months_entry`; helpers `set_active_since`, `opened_with_rent`; add `set_auto_paid_through(pool, date)`):
