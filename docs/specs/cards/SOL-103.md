# SOL-103 — Meu ano: the year's insights in one tab

Owner request (2026-11-12): a "Meu ano" tab showing what came in and what went out, the months with the most spending, the largest receipts, credit card spending, "esse tipo de coisas, insights do ano". The owner was unavailable, so the panel (product, finance, ux, architect) decided. Decisions are final. There is no Stitch frame, so the screen is built from the Soldi system: tokens, `PageHero`, cards, `Money`, `SplitBar`, `CategoryIcon`. Light only.

Plan: `docs/specs/plans/SOL-103.md`. New rule: D31.

## Panel outcome

### 1. Count spending by purchase date (D31)

Votes:

- **finance:** a card purchase belongs to the month it was made. A December purchase paid in January is December's spending. Paying the fatura is moving money, not spending it again. A deposit into Economia is saving, not consumption.
- **product:** year reviews (Nubank, Mobills, Monarch) count by purchase date, and so does Para onde vai (D28).
- **ux:** a user's top categories should add up to their spending total.
- **architect:** two `GROUP BY` queries using the filters that `category_sums` and `savings_by_month` already use.

Winner: count by purchase date. `Gastos` = Saída entries, except deposits into Economia and fatura payments, plus every Diário entry, card spends included.

Trade-offs:

- With cards or deposits, a month's `Gastos` on Meu ano differs from Mês `Saídas`. Mês counts the fatura payment and the deposits.
- A fatura paid above its itemized spends (fees, spends never typed in) is left out of `Gastos` (the D28 trade-off).
- The screen says `Gastos`, never `Saídas`. The card section states `Pela data da compra, não da fatura.`

Rejected:

- **Counting fatura payments:** the year would include last December's purchases and leave out this December's. Category totals would no longer add up to `Gastos`. D30's carries would leak into the numbers.
- **Counting both spends and payments:** spending counted twice, against D28.

### 2. The period: whole months up to today's month

Votes:

- **finance:** whole months, the same cut as the month view and Economia do ano, so `Entradas` and `Guardado` match those screens.
- **ux:** label the period, and never say "até hoje".

Winner: the year runs from January (or the opening month) to December. For the current year it stops at the end of today's month. Entries already dated later in today's month count, since the month view counts them too. Entries in later months never show here, because D16 permits them but nothing on this screen needs them.

Rejected:

- **Cutting at today:** Meu ano would disagree with the Economia do ano total on the next card down.
- **Forecast months:** not asked for (YAGNI).

### 3. The insights

The smallest set that answers the owner's request:

| # | Card | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Resumo do ano | "recebido e gasto", plus where the income went |
| 2 | Meses com mais gastos | "os meses com maiores gastos" |
| 3 | Onde você mais gastou | "esse tipo de coisas": the first insight in every year review |
| 4 | Maiores entradas | "os maiores recebimentos" |
| 5 | No cartão de crédito | "gastos de cartão" |

Rejected:

- **Monthly averages and "no ritmo atual":** D16 and D24 forbid them.
- **Comparison with last year:** a third query that nobody asked for.
- **Largest single purchase:** not asked for.
- **A 12-bar month chart:** speculative. Economia do ano already lists every month.
- **CSV export:** Economia do ano has one; nobody asked for one here.
- **"Maiores entradas" as the largest single entries:** with a monthly salary, the five largest entries are five copies of it. Grouping income by category answers "de onde veio".

### 4. Where the tab lives

Votes:

- **ux:** spec §1.5 fixes the mobile tab bar at 5 slots. Replacing a slot breaks it, and a 6th slot breaks the centered `+`. `Economia` is already the year screen (it has a year switcher), so Meu ano sits next to it as a tab.
- **product:** an "aba" one tap from a main tab is discoverable. An entry inside Ajustes is not, because Ajustes is settings.
- **architect:** the shell already owns navigation, so the sub-tabs live in `app-shell.tsx`. No new widget.

Winner:

- **Route** `/meu-ano`.
- **≥ 1024:** a sidebar `MENU` item `Meu ano` (lucide `CalendarRange`) between `Para onde vai` and `Cofrinhos`.
- **< 1024:** a two-link segmented nav under the app bar, on `/economia` and `/meu-ano` only: `Economia` | `Meu ano`. The `Economia` slot in the tab bar stays active on both pages.
- **App bar page name:** `Meu ano`.
- **Top bar:** `/meu-ano` does not read `?m=`, so it shows no month switcher (the same as `/posso-comprar`). The tier pill, `+ Lançar` and the avatar stay.

Rejected:

- **Replacing a tab:** breaks §1.5.
- **A 6th slot:** breaks §1.5's centered `+`.
- **A link in Ajustes:** Ajustes is settings, and the entry is hard to discover there.
- **A card on Mês:** Mês is a month dashboard (D25).

### 5. Default year

In January, when the opening year is before this one, the screen opens on last year. Otherwise it opens on this year. January is when people look back at a year, and the current year has only a few days by then.

## Endpoint

`GET /api/reports/year?year=YYYY` → `YearReport` (tag `reports`). It returns 400 without `year`, 422 when `year` is not 2000–2100, and 401 without a session. Besides the settings read, it runs two `GROUP BY` queries, both scoped by `user_id`:

1. **By month and card:** `year_flows`.
2. **By top-level category:** `year_groups`.

Nothing is stored.

```text
YearReport {
  year: i32,
  first_month: Option<String>,   // "2026-01"; null when nothing is counted (before the opening, after today's year)
  last_month:  Option<String>,   // "2026-11" (today's month) or "YYYY-12"
  totals: Totals,                // the month view's Totals shape, summed over the year by purchase date (D31):
                                 //   bills = Saída without fatura payments (Economia deposits inside, as in the month view),
                                 //   daily = every Diário entry (card spends included), saved = deposited − withdrawn
  spent: Decimal,                // Gastos = totals.bills − deposits + totals.daily
  top_months: Vec<YearMonth>,    // ≤ 3, spent > 0, spent desc, then oldest first
  spending: Vec<YearGroup>,      // ≤ 5 outflow groups but Economia, amount desc then name; pct of spent
  income: Vec<YearGroup>,        // ≤ 5 income groups, amount desc then name; pct of totals.income
  card_spent: Decimal,           // Diário entries with card_id
  card_pct: Option<Decimal>,     // card_spent over spent, one decimal half to even; null when spent is 0
  cards: Vec<YearCard>,          // every card with spends (archived ones too), amount desc then name
}
YearMonth { month: String, spent: Decimal }
YearGroup { category_id: Uuid, name: String, icon: String, color: String, amount: Decimal, pct: Decimal }
YearCard  { card_id: Uuid, name: String, amount: Decimal }
```

Identities, which tests must check:

- `spent` = Σ of every outflow group's amount (all groups, not just the top 5).
- `totals.income` = Σ of every income group's amount.
- `totals.saved`, `totals.income` and `totals.withdrawn` for a past year equal `/api/reports/savings`'s `saved_total`, `income_total` and `withdrawn_total`.

Filters, the same as the existing queries:

- **income:** `kind='income' AND cofrinho_id IS NULL`.
- **withdrawn:** `kind<>'bill' AND cofrinho_id IS NOT NULL`.
- **deposited:** `kind='bill' AND (c.is_savings OR p.is_savings OR cofrinho_id IS NOT NULL)`.
- **bills:** `kind='bill'`, not deposited, and `recurring_bills.closing_day IS NULL` on its `recurring_bill_id`.
- **daily:** `kind='daily' AND cofrinho_id IS NULL`.
- **groups:** `COALESCE(p.id, c.id)`, split by `t.kind='income'`, with resgates, deposits and fatura payments left out.

## Screen

Widget `widgets/year-review/year-review.tsx` (`YearReview`), page `app/(app)/(shell)/meu-ano/page.tsx`. Copy is pt-BR and verbatim.

### Page hero (always)

- **Component:** `PageHero`, level 1.
- **Eyebrow:** `RETROSPECTIVA`.
- **Title:** `Meu ano`.
- **Subtitle:** `O que entrou, o que saiu e para onde foi.`
- **Year switcher:** the `Switcher` from `shared/ui/month-switcher`, with legend `Ano`, `Ano anterior` and `Próximo ano`. It is bounded by the opening year and this year, as in `SavingsYear`.

### 1. Resumo do ano

Content: the year is not empty (see States).

- **Frame:** a section card titled `Resumo do ano`. Under it, the period in muted text:
  - `{Primeiro mês} a {último mês} de {ano}`, for example `Janeiro a novembro de 2026` or `Junho a dezembro de 2025`;
  - `{Mês} de {ano}` when a single month is counted, for example `Novembro de 2026`.
- **Two figures:**
  - `Entradas`: `Money income size="figure"`, rendered `+R$ …` in green.
  - `Gastos`: `Money outflow size="figure"`, rendered `- R$ …`.
  - Layout: stacked (label over amount) below 1024, two columns from 1024.
- **The split, from `splitOf(totals)`:**
  - Heading row: `Para onde foi a entrada do ano` and, at the right, `Total: {base}`.
  - `SplitBar` label `Entradas`, or `Saídas` when overspent. Segments as on Mês: `Contas fixas`, `Guardado`, `Gastos diários`, `Livre restante`.
  - The split shows at every width; on Mês it shows only from 1024.
  - When there is no income, `splitOf` returns null and the split is absent. The figures stay.

### 2. Meses com mais gastos

Content: `top_months.length ≥ 2`.

- **Title:** `Meses com mais gastos`.
- **Rows:** an ordered list of up to 3 rows, each a link to `/para-onde-vai?m={YYYY-MM}`, ≥ 44 px tall.
  - Left to right: `1º`/`2º`/`3º` (muted, tabular figures), the month name (`Março`), then `Money outflow` at the right and an aria-hidden `›`.
  - Under the row, an 8 px bar in `bg-expense`, as wide as the month's cents relative to the top month's (100% for the top month).
  - The link's accessible name is its text: `1º Março - R$ 7.200,00`.

### 3. Onde você mais gastou

Content: `spending.length ≥ 1`.

- **Header:** title `Onde você mais gastou`; caption at the right, `Por grupo, no ano`.
- **Rows:** up to 5, not links. Each row has:
  - the `CategoryIcon` in the group color, the name, and the share `({pct})` in muted text;
  - `Money outflow` at the right;
  - under it, an 8 px bar in `categoryBg(color)` at `pct%` width, as in Para onde vai's Top 5 (`category-breakdown.tsx:172`).
- **Economia never appears here.** It is saving, shown as `Guardado` in the split.

### 4. Maiores entradas

Content: `income.length ≥ 1`.

- **Header:** title `Maiores entradas`; caption `Por categoria, no ano`.
- **Rows:** the same row as card 3, with `Money income` (`+R$`, green).
- With a single income group the card still shows its one row at `100%`. That row is content (D25).

### 5. No cartão de crédito

Content: `card_spent > 0`.

- **Title:** `No cartão de crédito`.
- **Body:**
  - `Money outflow size="figure"` for `card_spent`;
  - the line `{card_pct} dos seus gastos no ano`, for example `32,5% dos seus gastos no ano`;
  - the muted caption `Pela data da compra, não da fatura.`;
  - with `cards.length ≥ 2`, a list with one row per card: a `CreditCard` icon, the name and `Money outflow`. One card shows no list, because the figure already is that card.

### States

| State | When | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| Loading | first fetch, or settings not loaded yet | Page hero only. No skeleton (D25). Switching years keeps the previous year on screen until the new one arrives (`keepPreviousData`). |
| Error | the year query fails with no data | Page hero, then `PageError` `Não foi possível carregar o ano.` with `Tentar de novo`, which refetches. |
| Empty year | `first_month` is null, or `totals.income`, `totals.outflow` and `totals.withdrawn` are all 0 | Page hero, then the centered muted line `Nada para mostrar em {ano}.` No cards. |
| First months | one month counted (opened this month, or January) | Resumo, Onde, Entradas and Cartão as content allows. Meses com mais gastos is absent, since it needs 2 months. |
| Overspent | `performance + withdrawn < 0` | The split's base is `Saídas`, and `Total` shows `totals.outflow`. There is no `Livre restante` segment. |
| No income | `totals.income` = 0, spending > 0 | Resumo shows `Entradas +R$ 0,00` and `Gastos`. There is no split, and Maiores entradas is absent. |
| No cards | `card_spent` = 0 | No cartão card. |
| One card | `cards.length` = 1 | Figure, share and caption, with no per-card list. |
| Full past year | | Period `Janeiro a dezembro de {ano}` (or from the opening month). All cards that have content. |

## Acceptance

- `GET /api/reports/year?year=2026` returns the shape above.
  - The response for a user with income, Saída, Diário, an Economia deposit, a resgate, a card with spends and a paid fatura counts the card spends once (on their purchase date). It leaves the fatura payment out of `spent`, `totals.bills` and every group, and leaves Economia out of `spending`.
  - Another user's entries never change the response.
  - 400 without a year, 422 for `1999` or `abcd`, 401 without a session.
  - 10,000 entries in a year come back in under 1 s.
- `spent` equals the sum of all outflow groups. For a past year, `totals.saved`, `totals.income` and `totals.withdrawn` equal the savings report's totals.
- The current year counts up to the end of today's month and ignores later months. A year before the opening date, or after today's year, comes back with null months, zeros and empty lists.
- The sidebar shows `Meu ano` between `Para onde vai` and `Cofrinhos`, active on `/meu-ano`.
- At < 1024:
  - `/economia` and `/meu-ano` show the segmented `Economia` / `Meu ano` nav, with the current one `aria-current="page"`;
  - the tab bar still has 5 slots, with `Economia` active on both pages;
  - the app bar reads `Meu ano`.
- Every state in the table renders as specified. Each card renders nothing when it has no content.
- Every amount comes from the API string through `Money`, and bar widths are computed in cents. There is no float math on money.
- No average, pace or projection appears on the screen.
- 375, 1024, 1280 and 1440, light, with seven-digit amounts: no horizontal scroll, no clipped amount, 44 px targets.
