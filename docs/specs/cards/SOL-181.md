## Hero (month hero, Saldo hoje on mobile, auth/onboarding top)

The indigo gradient (`--hero-from`, `--hero-to`) goes away. Default: the hero becomes the card surface (white, `shadow-sm`, dark text), which is what `month-overview` already does from `lg`. Alternative if Mês reads flat at 375: brand soft `#e6f1ee`, where income text needs a darker green (`#15803d` on it is 4.34:1). The designer picks one, the owner sees Mês at 375 and 1440 before merge.

- Replace `from-hero-from to-hero-to bg-linear-to-br` and `text-hero-foreground*` in `shared/ui/hero-frame.tsx`, `widgets/month-header/month-header.tsx`, `widgets/month-overview/month-overview.tsx`, `features/welcome-tour/welcome-tour.tsx`; drop the three `--hero-*` tokens from the preset and `globals.css`.
