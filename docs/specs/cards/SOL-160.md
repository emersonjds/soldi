# SOL-160 — A Conta series becomes a Conta fixa or a Conta parcelada, by choice

Owner, production 0.66.0, 2026-11-23: "Casa › Financiamento" (R$ 2.339,68, 16 nov) is still a 2-member Conta series ("Saída · 1/2"), and it is really a conta fixa. Vivo and Vivo Mãe (26 nov) are likely the same case. Owner model (2026-11-23, closed): a Conta is a **Conta fixa** (every month, no end) or a **Conta parcelada** (N months), both `recurring_bills`. A series converts only when the person edits it. There is no data migration.

Root cause, from the code: the small link "Criar como Conta Fixa" opens Nova conta with **Parcelado on** for any series that runs through this month. The Parcelado switch already shows with a prefill: line 241 hides only the Cartão switch, not the choice. But nothing says that Parcelado off means "conta fixa, sem fim", and the title, the pill, the button and the toast all say "conta fixa" while a parcelada is saved. Getting a fixa needs a hidden step (turn Parcelado off).

Web only (`transaction-sheet.tsx`, `edit-bill-sheet.tsx`). The server already takes either body on a series member and does not check dates (`check_series_convertible`). No contract change, no new component, no new token.

## What stays exactly as today

- "Editar Lançamento Recorrente": Escopo, Valor por parcela, Quantas vezes with Ciclo Atual (resize), Dia de vencimento, Categoria, Observação, Excluir da Série..., and Salvar Alterações na Série as the one primary action.
- Lançar and a stored standalone Conta keep the SOL-156 "Conta fixa" / "Parcelado" switches. Their Parcelado still writes a series, and SOL-162 changes that.
- Contas fixas (Ajustes) and its BillForm: same switches, labels and button, "Salvar Conta Fixa" included.
- `prefillOf` math (D20 day, `installments`, `last_month`), the cut at the end of this month, the 422 texts, and no undo (D44).

## Changes

1. **Entry point.** The link "Prefere transformar em conta fixa recorrente? Criar como Conta Fixa →" is replaced, in the same place (under Quantas vezes), by a group:
   - caption "Transformar em conta";
   - two outline buttons side by side, each ≥ 44 px tall with 16 px text: **"Conta fixa"** (`Repeat` icon) and **"Conta parcelada"** (`Layers` icon), the icons of the SOL-156 switches;
   - one helper line: "Fixa: todo mês, sem fim. Parcelada: termina em {mmm/aa}." ({mmm/aa} is `monthAbbrev` of `series.last_date`).
   - It is offered only on a member with kind `bill`, `series.every === "month"`, no `recurring_bill_id`, no `cofrinho_id`, no `card_id`, and a bill sheet host (`onConvert`). It is never on a weekly or yearly member, a payment, a resgate, a card spend or a non-Conta.
2. **Series that ended before this month.** Only "Conta fixa" shows, full width, with the helper "A série terminou em {mmm/aa}. Como conta fixa, volta a prever todo mês." All its members become payments, nothing is deleted, and the conta owes from this month on (D17), like any new conta fixa.
3. **Nova conta opens with the chosen kind.** "Conta fixa" opens with Parcelado **off**, and "Conta parcelada" opens with it **on**. Both carry today's `prefillOf` (name, amount, D20 day, category, and the plan whenever the series runs through this month). Turning Parcelado on after Conta fixa shows the same Quantas parcelas / Parcela atual numbers. Turning it off sends no `installments` and no `last_month`. (ponytail: one optional `parcelado` flag on the sheet, read by `draftOf` before `installments != null`.)
4. **The hint follows the switch** (it replaces `SERIES_HINT`):
   - Fixa, with members after this month: "Os lançamentos desta série até este mês viram pagamentos desta conta. Os dos próximos meses saem da lista: a conta fixa prevê todo mês, sem fim."
   - Fixa, with no member after this month (it ends this month or ended earlier): "Os lançamentos desta série viram pagamentos desta conta. Daqui em diante, a conta fixa prevê todo mês, sem fim."
   - Parcelada running past this month: "Os lançamentos desta série até este mês viram pagamentos desta conta. Os dos próximos meses saem da lista: a conta prevê cada parcela até {mmm/aa}."
   - Parcelada ending this month: "Os lançamentos desta série viram pagamentos desta conta. A última parcela é deste mês."
5. **Toasts.** A body with `installments` gives "Conta parcelada criada", and one without gives "Conta fixa criada" (Lançar's Conta fixa is unchanged). Errors are the fixed SOL-152 texts: `series-shared-month` → "Esta série tem dois lançamentos no mesmo mês. Exclua um deles e tente de novo."; anything else → "Não foi possível criar a conta fixa.".

## Research (the exact step: change a repeating expense's kind while editing it)

- **Organizze:** editing a fixo or parcelado entry only asks "este e os próximos" / "somente este". There is no switch between fixo and parcelado, so the person deletes and re-enters it ([help](https://ajuda.organizze.com.br/hc/pt-br/articles/5882498323347)). Creating offers two plain words, "despesa fixa" / "parcelado em" (`docs/research/2026-11-14-market-benchmark.md` §5).
- **Mobills:** "Editar somente esta" / "Editar todas (incluindo pagas)". Every field except date and status can be edited, and the repeat type cannot ([help](https://mobills.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050084953)).
- **YNAB:** a scheduled transaction's "Repeat" field is edited in place in the same form: one tap, one pick ([guide](https://support.ynab.com/en_us/scheduled-transactions-a-guide-BygrAIFA9)).
- Takeaway: the choice belongs in the edit sheet, in place and in plain words (YNAB), using Organizze's two words. The BR apps make it a delete and re-enter, and Soldi already beats that.

## D45 — A series member converts to the kind the person picks (2026-11-23, owner + SOL-160; amends D44)

On a monthly Conta series member, the person picks **Conta fixa** or **Conta parcelada** before Nova conta opens. A Conta fixa body (no `installments`, no `last_month`) is now allowed for a bounded series, because the person chose it: the conta owes every month after the series' last member. D44 rejected that only as a default the web chose by itself. A series that ended before this month may convert to a Conta fixa: all its members become payments and nothing is deleted. A parcelada keeps D44's prefill. The server is unchanged. Trade-off: Próximos meses after the series' end now owe the conta, which is the person's explicit choice.
