# SOL-162 — Lançar a Conta Parcelado makes a conta parcelada

Owner, 2026-11-23 (closed): "Lançar Conta + Parcelado (no card) creates a conta parcelada. Today's entry is the paid parcela, and the later months show in Contas a pagar as unpaid. A card purchase in parcelas stays as it is." Today a monthly Parcelado Conta writes a transaction series (`repeat_months`, or `installments` + `first_installment` with the total), which contradicts the owner model (a Conta is a fixa or a parcelada, both `recurring_bills`).

Web only (`transaction-sheet.tsx`). The server already takes a standalone `bill` entry plus a plan on `POST /api/bills?transaction_id=` (R8 path, `check_plan`). No contract change, no new component, no new token.

## What stays exactly as today

- The Conta fixa switch: Salvar opens Nova conta, then Salvar Conta Fixa (SOL-149, SOL-156). Same 3 taps.
- Entrada and Diário "Recorrente" (salário Sempre, card subscriptions, card purchases in parcelas, Começa na parcela with D16's total split): unchanged.
- Editing a series member (SOL-160 "Transformar em conta"), a payment ("Editar conta", SOL-161), a resgate: unchanged.
- Weekly and yearly Parcelado on a Conta, and dates outside the window below, still write a series as today.

## Changes

1. **Flow (no second sheet).** On a Conta with Parcelado on, Mensal, a valid count, and an entry date in **this month or next month**, Salvar Lançamento closes the sheet and:
   - posts the plain entry (`POST /api/transactions`, no repeat keys);
   - then `POST /api/bills?transaction_id=<entry>` with `prefillOf(entry)` (name = note or category name, amount, `due_day` = entry day, `category_id`) plus `installments` = N and `last_month` = first day of (entry month + N − current).
   - Editar of a stored standalone Conta: same rule (current is always 1, no Começa na parcela there). It `PATCH`es the entry with the edits and no repeat keys, then posts the bill with `transaction_id` = the stored entry.
2. **Amount = the parcela.** The typed value is one parcela and is the bill's `amount`, as the summary line already reads ("3× de R$ 100,00"). With Parcelado on, the amount label reads **"Valor da parcela"** (every frequency). No total is sent and nothing is split: D16's "parcelas split a total" stays for the series paths only (Diário/Entrada Recorrente with Começa na parcela). R$ 1.000,00 in 3: the person types 333,33, the summary shows "3× de R$ 333,33"; the cent left over is typed on that parcela's payment entry. A bill has one amount a month (D35), so a remainder parcela cannot be stored.
3. **Parcela atual.** "Começa na parcela" k makes today's entry parcela k: `last_month` = entry month + (N − k). Parcelas before k are not written (as today). The entry is its month's payment (SOL-155 rule: linked = paid, dated or not), so a later day this month or a date next month reads as paid.
4. **Window.** This month or next month always passes `check_plan` (`this ≤ last_month ≤ this + N`). Other dates stay a series: a past month (back-filling history, where D17 would owe nothing before today) and two or more months ahead (the plan would start after next month, 422). The DateChips `min` (opening date) is unchanged.
5. **Caption** (muted, under the summary line, only when the conta parcelada path applies):
   - k < N: "Vira uma conta parcelada: este lançamento paga a parcela {k} e as próximas ficam em Contas a pagar até {mmm/aa}."
   - k = N: "Vira uma conta parcelada: este lançamento paga a última parcela."
   - No caption on a series path; the summary line alone tells it, as today.
6. **Toasts.** Success "Conta parcelada criada" (SOL-160 string). Entry fails: "Não foi possível salvar o lançamento." and no bill call. Bill fails after the entry saved: "O lançamento foi salvo, mas a conta parcelada não foi criada. Tente de novo em Editar." Server text is never shown. No undo (D44); the person reviews later through the payment's "Editar conta" (SOL-161).

## D47 draft — Lançar's monthly Parcelado on a Conta is a conta parcelada (2026-11-23, owner + SOL-162; extends D35, D45)

A new or standalone Conta with Parcelado, Mensal and an entry in this month or the next one saves the entry, then creates a `recurring_bills` parcelada linked to it (R8): `amount` = the typed parcela, `installments` = N, `last_month` = entry month + N − Começa na parcela. Weekly, yearly, past-month and later dates keep D14's series. Card purchases in parcelas are untouched. Trade-off: the result depends on frequency and date; the caption says when it is a conta.
