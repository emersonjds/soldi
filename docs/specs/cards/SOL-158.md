# SOL-158 — Collapse Contas a pagar and Lançamentos

Owner request 2026-11-22: a chevron at the right of the Contas a pagar and Lançamentos headers on `/mes` that collapses or expands the card, with the choice kept so the person decides whether these cards always show open or closed.

Decisions (owner's request; no panel, no new token):
- The chevron is a 44 px button named after the card, with `aria-expanded`; it points down when collapsed and up when open. Only the chevron toggles; the title stays a heading.
- Both cards start open when nothing is remembered, so nobody who never taps the chevron sees a change.
- Collapsed Contas a pagar keeps the title, the paid count and the overdue / Próxima line, the at-a-glance part; the day chips, the groups, the rows and Gerenciar contas fixas hide.
- Collapsed Lançamentos keeps the title; the filters and the days hide.
- The choice lives in this browser's `localStorage` (`soldi:open:bills`, `soldi:open:ledger`), not in the account. Blocked storage falls back to open and still toggles for the visit.
- Ver o dia in the red-day alert opens a collapsed Lançamentos for that visit to land on the day, without changing the remembered choice.

Acceptance (covered in `bills-of-month.test.tsx` and `mes/page.test.tsx`):
- Open by default; the chevron collapses to the states above and the choice survives a reload, per card.
- A remembered "closed" opens collapsed; the chevron opens it and remembers "open".
- Blocked storage: open, and the chevron still toggles.
- Jump from the alert into a collapsed Lançamentos focuses the day and leaves `soldi:open:ledger` at "closed".

Follow-up if wanted: keep the choice in the account (`/api/settings`) so it follows the person across devices.
