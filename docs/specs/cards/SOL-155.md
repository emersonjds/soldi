# SOL-155 — A conta fixa made from a future-dated Lançar entry

Owner report 2026-11-22: Gás (R$ 177,31, 30/11) created through Lançar > Recorrente > Salvar showed under Contas a pagar as "0 de 1 pagas" with Pagar, and Lançamentos listed "Gás · Conta fixa · a pagar".

Finding: not reproducible on 0.64.1.
- API: a `bill` entry dated after today, converted with `POST /api/bills?transaction_id=…`, is linked (`recurring_bill_id` set); the month marks the bill paid with `lands_on: null`, and the due day's projection takes the amount once.
- Web: the e2e `a future Conta on Recorrente Sempre becomes a conta fixa paid on its day, counted once` (`web/e2e/repeat.spec.ts`) drives the real flow and asserts "1 de 1 pagas", one Gás row on its day, no "Conta fixa · a pagar" row, and the day's projected balance at 1.000,00 − 177,31. Without the `transactionId` on the bill request it fails with `paid: false`.

Rule (unchanged): an entry linked to a conta fixa is that month's payment, dated or not, so the bill is paid on the entry's date and the projection counts the entry only. There is no "scheduled" state.

Likely cause of the owner's data: the Gás entry and the Gás conta fixa are not linked, which happens when the conta fixa was created separately (Contas fixas) or the conversion failed ("Lançado, mas a conta fixa não foi criada"). Unlinked, the bill lands as unpaid on its due day while the standalone entry also counts, so the day takes 177,31 twice.

Data fix for the owner: delete the Gás conta fixa, then open the Gás entry > Editar > "Criar como Conta Fixa", which links the stored entry.

Follow-up worth a card: offering to link a standalone Saída to an unpaid conta fixa with the same amount in the same month, instead of the user re-creating it.
