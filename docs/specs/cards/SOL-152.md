# SOL-152 — Contas mensais: a Conta series becomes a conta fixa; a conta fixa may vary

Owner, 2026-11-22. Contas a pagar lists only contas fixas (`recurring_bills`). The owner typed Luz, Vivo, Vivo Mãe, Celular, Psicóloga and his cards (Porto Black, C6, Cartão Mais) as recurring Conta series (`transactions` sharing a `series_id`), so none of them shows there. To him there is one kind of thing: a monthly bill due on the same day, whose amount is either fixed (Psicóloga, a financing) or variable (Luz, água, internet, a card he keeps using).

Today a conta fixa already carries an estimated `amount`, and Pagar adjusts the month's amount. A Conta series is rows only. Editing a series member offers "Criar como Conta Fixa", but the server refuses it (`transactions::domain::check_convertible` accepts only a standalone entry), so the link is a dead end. The same link also shows on a bill payment, which the server refuses too.

Out of scope: the Contas a pagar layout (SOL-153), Lançamentos filters (SOL-154).

## 1. Current code this card builds on

- `bills::service::create(pool, user, input, transaction_id)` (R8, SOL-21): with `transaction_id`, `transactions::repo::link_bill` turns one standalone `bill` entry into the new bill's payment in the same DB transaction and moves it to the bill's category.
- A bill owes a month when its due date is on or after the opening date and on or after `active_since`, inside its run (`months::domain::owed`, D17, D35). A new bill's `active_since` is today (São Paulo, D13), so months before today never owe.
- A payment is any entry with `recurring_bill_id`; one per bill and month (`transactions_bill_month_idx`). SOL-155 fixed the rule that an entry linked to a conta fixa is that month's payment whether its date is past or future.
- Meu ano counts entries up to the end of today's month (`reports::domain::counted_range`). Para onde vai counts entries by their own category (`reports::repo::category_sums`). Próximos meses is the forecast: projected balances from entries plus unpaid bills (D16, D17).
- A series has no flag telling parcelas from a plain repeat (D19). `series.total` counts from parcela 1 (`series_offset` included).
- `open_ended` is refused on a `bill` entry (D36), so an open Conta series cannot be created.
- `BillForm` (`web/src/features/edit-bill/edit-bill-sheet.tsx`) hides the Cartão and Parcelado switches when it has a `prefill`.
- On a new bill made from a prefill, `submit` sends `installments`/`last_month` only when they differ from the initial draft. Today no prefill carries them, so the bug is latent.

## 2. Decisions

### 2.1 Fixed or variable is a stored flag

- `recurring_bills.variable boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false`.
- `CHECK (closing_day IS NULL OR NOT variable)`: a card's fatura already varies, so a card is never variable.
- `variable` is added to `Bill`, `CreateBill` (`#[serde(default)]`), `UpdateBill` (`Option<bool>`) and `BillOfMonth`.
- `bills::domain::check` refuses `variable: true` with `closing_day` with 422 `validation` on field `variable`, message `a card's fatura already varies`.
- `amount` stays the estimate for both kinds. Projection, D17, D35 and the forecast do not read the flag.
- Onboarding (`insert_all`) stores the flag too. It costs one more `UNNEST` column, and a conta fixa sent with `variable: true` must not lose it.

### 2.2 A monthly Conta series converts on the same endpoint

`POST /api/bills?transaction_id=<id>` keeps its query parameter. When `<id>` is a series member, the server converts the whole series. The web already sends this request.

The body is authoritative: it is the bill exactly as the form shows it. The server does not derive name, amount, day or category from the series. The web prefills them (§2.4).

The conversion runs in one database transaction:

1. `transactions::repo::find` loads the entry; it is 404 when missing or another user's (unchanged).
2. Without a series, the R8 path runs unchanged (`check_convertible` → `link_bill`, which moves the entry to the bill's category).
3. With a series, `transactions::domain::check_series_convertible(&entry)` runs. It needs kind `bill`, no `recurring_bill_id`, no `cofrinho_id`, no `card_id` and `series.every == Month`; anything else answers 422 `cannot-become-bill`.
4. `transactions::repo::lock_series(series.id)`: the same advisory lock growth and resizes take.
5. `transactions::repo::series_blockers(user, series, through)`, where `through` is the end of today's São Paulo month, returns two facts:
   - `mixed`: some member is not a `bill` entry or carries a card. This answers 422 `cannot-become-bill`.
   - `shared_month`: two members dated through `through` share a month. This answers 422 `series-shared-month`.
6. The bill is inserted, with the same checks as today (`check`, `check_plan`, rule 11).
7. `transactions::repo::link_series(user, series, bill, through)`:
   - deletes the members dated after `through`;
   - sets `recurring_bill_id = bill` on the rest and detaches them (`series_id`, `series_every` NULL; `series_open` false; `series_offset` 0);
   - leaves date, amount, note and category untouched.
   A unique violation (a concurrent payment) maps through `transactions::service::paid_once` to 409 `bill-already-paid`.
8. Commit.

New problem: `series-shared-month`, 422, detail `Two entries of this series fall in one month; delete one first.`

### 2.3 Which members become payments: the cut is the end of this month

Members dated on or before the last day of today's São Paulo month become payments. Members in later months are deleted.

### 2.4 Web prefill and copy

The link "Criar como Conta Fixa" shows on a stored entry only when all of these hold:

- kind `bill`;
- no `recurring_bill_id` (this removes today's dead link on a bill payment);
- no `cofrinho_id`;
- no series, or a series with `every === "month"`.

In Lançar (no stored entry) it shows as today.

The prefill for a series member extends today's `prefillOf`:

- **Name, amount, category:** the opened member's (`note ?? category.name`, `amount`, `category.id`), as today.
- **Due day:** the D20 series day (`transactions::domain::series_day`), using `series.last_date` and the opened member's date as "the one before". It is `last_date`'s day, or the larger of the two days when `last_date` is its month's last day. In the month of the last member, the due day then never falls before that member, so the form's `Parcela atual` round-trips to the same `last_month`.
- **Parcelado:** when the series is bounded and `series.last_date` falls after this month, the prefill sends `installments: series.total` and `last_month: <last_date's month>-01`. Otherwise it is a Fixo with no end. The user can flip the Parcelado switch, which now shows with a prefill; the Cartão switch still does not.
- **Variable:** off. The user turns it on in the form.

`EditBillSheet` gains an optional `hint`, rendered like `TransactionSheet`'s `hint`. The convert flow passes it for a series member:

> Os lançamentos desta série até este mês viram pagamentos desta conta. Os dos próximos meses saem da lista: a conta passa a prever cada mês.

Submit is the confirmation. No extra dialog, no undo (§4).

Results:

- **Success:** the toast "Conta fixa criada" (unchanged).
- **Failure:** `problemMessage(error)`, else the existing fixed text ("Não foi possível criar a conta fixa." in Editar). Server text is never shown. New `PROBLEMS` entry in `web/src/shared/api/client.ts`:
  - `series-shared-month` → "Esta série tem dois lançamentos no mesmo mês. Exclua um deles e tente de novo."

`BillForm` gains a switch after the Valor field. It is hidden when Cartão de crédito is on:

- Label: **Valor varia todo mês**
- Helper, only when on: "Ex.: luz, água, cartão. O valor fica como estimativa e você ajusta ao pagar."
- When on, the Valor label reads **Valor estimado**; off, it stays "Valor mensal fixo estimado".
- The form sends `variable` only when it differs from the stored value (`false` for a new bill), so existing request bodies stay the same.

The variable mark:

- **Contas a pagar** (`widgets/bills-of-month`), on an unpaid variable row:
  - the amount reads `≈ R$ 180,00`, the `≈` `aria-hidden`;
  - the name line adds `· valor variável`, styled like the parcela text;
  - the adjust button's label starts with `valor estimado ` (`valor estimado R$ 180,00: ajustar pagamento de Luz`);
  - "Próxima: dia 25 · ≈ R$ 180,00" when the next bill is variable.
  A paid row shows the paid amount with no mark.
- **Contas fixas** (`widgets/bill-list`): `≈` before the amount and `· valor variável` in the name line, always.
- **Pagar:** the round check and Pagar on an unpaid variable row open the Pagar sheet with the estimate prefilled, as a card's fatura does today (`payNow`: `bill.is_card || bill.variable`). One tap never books the estimate as if it were exact.

## 3. Challenges to the orchestrator's decisions (code-backed)

1. **Cut at the end of this month, not at today.** With "date ≤ today", a member dated later this month would be deleted. Mês Saídas, Meu ano (counted through today's month end) and Para onde vai of this month would then drop by it, which breaks the card's own acceptance ("totals do not change"). SOL-155's rule already makes a linked future-dated entry its month's payment, and R8 converts a future-dated standalone entry the same way. Cost: a member dated later this month reads as paid. For a variable bill the owner taps the check to undo, then pays the real amount; that happens once, in the conversion month.
2. **Payments keep their own category.** `link_bill` moves the entry to the bill's category. A series' members can differ (`scope=this` edits one), and the form lets the user pick another category. Moving them would rewrite Para onde vai history, which D35 forbids. `link_series` leaves `category_id` alone. R8's standalone path is unchanged.
3. **The body decides the bill; the web prefills from the opened member.** The web holds one member, not "the latest member on or before today". Deriving fields server-side would ignore what the form shows. An extra read endpoint is not worth a default the user sees and edits anyway.
4. **A bounded series is prefilled Parcelado, not a Fixo with no end.** The server cannot tell parcelas from a repeat (D19). A Fixo with no end would owe every month after the series' last member, so Próximos meses would move. Parcelado with `last_month` = the last member's month keeps the same months. The switch lets the owner choose Fixo for a card he keeps using.
5. **No open-ended Conta series exists.** `open_ended` 422s on a `bill` entry (D36), so the "open-ended becomes Fixo" case cannot be reached. It is still safe:
   - `link_series` runs under `lock_series`;
   - `extend_open_series` re-reads the last member under the same lock, and a detached or deleted member ends that loop (`continue`);
   - `insert_copies` copies `series_open` from a remaining member, and none remains.
6. **No refusal for Economia or a cofrinho.** R8 already turns a standalone Saída on Economia into a conta fixa. Deposits are counted by category (`savings_by_month`, `year_flows`, `months::repo::saved`), not by bill, and payments keep their category (2), so Economia's totals do not move. The web also cannot see a cofrinho's parent flag on the entry. A card entry is already impossible on kind `bill` (`check_card`), and `mixed` covers stale data.
7. **Migration is forward-only.** Every migration in `crates/soldi-server/migrations` is a single forward file. Revert:

   ```sql
   ALTER TABLE recurring_bills DROP CONSTRAINT recurring_bills_card_not_variable;
   ALTER TABLE recurring_bills DROP COLUMN variable;
   ```

   This follows 0026's convention.
8. **Two problem types, not one per cause.** The web hides the weekly, yearly, payment and resgate cases before the request. Only `series-shared-month` is one the web cannot see, so only it gets its own type and copy. The rest share `cannot-become-bill`.

## 4. Which totals can still move (accepted)

Unchanged, exactly:

- every month up to and including this one in Mês: totals, days, balance and projected balance. Contas a pagar of those months now also lists the bill, paid, or unpaid and not overdue in a gap month;
- Meu ano;
- Para onde vai for those months;
- Economia do ano;
- `balance_today`.

Their rows keep date, amount and category, and they are still counted by every report. Only `recurring_bill_id` and the series columns change.

Future months, which can move:

- **Mês Saídas, Para onde vai and Economia of a future month** lose the deleted member. Its amount moves from a recorded entry to the bill's unpaid projection, so the month's `projected_balance` and every Próximos meses balance stay equal **when the bill amount equals the deleted member's amount**.
- **Projected balances move by the difference** when:
  - members differ from the bill amount (parcelas with the cents remainder on the last one, a member edited alone, a variable bill's typed values);
  - a future month had no member (the bill now owes it);
  - the user switches to Fixo past the series' end, or edits the day, name or category in the form.
- **This month with no member** (a gap) whose due day is today or later: the bill now owes it (D17), so this month's projection drops by the bill amount. A past month without a member stays as it was. It is before `active_since`, so it never owes.

Undo: none. Re-creating the deleted members is not offered; it would be a second series editor. Excluir on the new bill archives it and keeps its payments as entries (unchanged rule), so past months stay exact even then.

## 5. Panel (one agent; product, ux, finance in turn; architect challenges)

| Question | product | ux | finance | Winner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Where the variable flag lives | bool on the bill | bool, the form says it in words | bool; the estimate stays the projected amount | stored `variable` |
| How a variable bill reads | "valor variável" | `≈` + "valor variável" while unpaid | the paid amount is never marked | `≈` + text while unpaid; Contas fixas always |
| Tapping the check on a variable bill | pay at once | open Pagar with the estimate | open Pagar: never book an estimate as exact | open Pagar |
| Cut for payments | today | today (Contas a pagar shows it to pay) | end of this month (totals and SOL-155) | **end of this month** (architect: acceptance plus SOL-155) |
| Bounded series default | Fixo | whatever the form shows, switchable | Parcelado (Próximos meses unchanged) | Parcelado, switchable |
| Confirmation | sheet text | sheet text; no extra dialog (one tap less) | no undo is acceptable since past months are exact | hint text in Nova conta |

Trade-off: the conversion month may show a member dated later this month as already paid. Everything before it stays exact, and nothing after it is double-counted.

## 6. Acceptance criteria (every state)

Server:

- **A1. Porto 24x converts.** A monthly Conta series of 24 starting three months ago on day 1, converted with a Parcelado body (`installments` 24, `last_month` the last member's month):
  - answers 201 with one bill due on day 1;
  - the members of the past three months and of this month are that bill's payments, with no series and unchanged date, amount and category;
  - no entry of the series remains after this month;
  - `GET /api/bills` lists the bill.
- **A2. Totals hold.** For each past month and this month, `GET /api/months/{m}` totals and `projected_balance` are equal before and after. So are `GET /api/reports/year?year=<this year>` and `GET /api/reports/categories?month=<this month>`. Next month's `projected_balance` is also equal, because the bill amount equals the members'.
- **A3. Today's member.** A series starting today becomes a bill paid today. Its later members are gone.
- **A4. A past gap.** A past month whose member was deleted shows the bill unpaid and not overdue, with `lands_on` null. Its totals are unchanged.
- **A5. A shared month.** Two members in one month up to this one (one moved there with `scope=this`) answer 422 `series-shared-month`. Nothing is written: no bill, and every member is intact.
- **A6. Other refusals.** A weekly series member, a yearly one, a bill payment and a resgate answer 422 `cannot-become-bill`. Another user's member answers 404. Nothing is written in either case.
- **A7. No regrowth.** After the conversion, reading a future month view (which runs `extend_open_series`) and the forecast writes no entry for the old series.
- **A8. Variable on a conta fixa.** `variable: true` on a conta fixa round-trips through create, list, patch and the month view (`bills_of_month[].variable`). `variable: true` on a card answers 422 `validation` on `variable`. An absent `variable` stays `false`.
- **A9. R8 unchanged.** A standalone Saída still becomes a bill and moves to the bill's category. The R8 refusal test now uses a weekly member instead of a monthly one.

Web:

- **W1. The link shows only where the server accepts.** It shows in Editar on a monthly Conta series member and on a standalone Conta. It is absent on a weekly or yearly member, on a bill payment, on a resgate and on a non-Conta entry. In Lançar it behaves as today.
- **W2. The prefill.** Criar como Conta Fixa on a bounded member whose series runs past this month opens Nova conta with:
  - the hint text (§2.4);
  - name, amount and category from the member;
  - the D20 due day;
  - Parcelado on, with `Quantas parcelas` = `series.total` and `Parcela atual` matching the series.
  Criar conta posts `/api/bills?transaction_id=<member>` with `installments` and `last_month`. The success toast is "Conta fixa criada".
- **W3. A Fixo prefill.** A member of a series that ends this month or earlier opens with Parcelado off and sends neither `installments` nor `last_month`.
- **W4. A refusal shows fixed text.** 422 `series-shared-month` shows "Esta série tem dois lançamentos no mesmo mês. Exclua um deles e tente de novo.". Any other failure shows "Não foi possível criar a conta fixa.".
- **W5. The variable switch.** "Valor varia todo mês" shows for a conta fixa (new, edit, prefilled) and never with Cartão de crédito on. On, it shows the helper and the "Valor estimado" label, and sends `variable: true`. Editing a variable bill opens with it on, and turning it off sends `variable: false`. Untouched, no `variable` key is sent.
- **W6. Contas a pagar.**
  - An unpaid variable row shows `≈`, "valor variável" and the adjust label starting `valor estimado`.
  - Its round check and Pagar open the Pagar sheet with the estimate, and no request leaves until Pagar is confirmed.
  - A paid variable row shows the paid amount unmarked.
  - A fixed bill behaves as today.
- **W7. Contas fixas** shows `≈` and "valor variável" on a variable bill and nothing new on a fixed one or a card.
- **W8. 375 px first:** the switch row is at least 44 px tall, no horizontal scroll, the `≈` amount does not wrap the row. Light only.
- **W9. e2e.** Seed a 24x Conta series from three months ago, open a past member in Lançamentos and convert it with "Valor varia todo mês" on. Then:
  - Mês shows the bill in Contas a pagar with `≈`;
  - the past month's totals read the same as before (API);
  - Lançamentos lists no member after this month.
