Owner rules that bound this epic:

- The Mês hero stays as shipped (SOL-69).
- Light only (D21).
- No importer (D18).
- No credit cards or fatura, no couple split.
- No AI callouts, no Soldi Pro, no CDI yield.
- Money is `Decimal`, BRL, and every query is filtered by `user_id`.

Everything Stitch shows that breaks a rule, duplicates a shipped feature, or needs a guess (D16) is listed with its reason in the README and is not specified here.

Common to every section:

- Money on screen goes through `Money` / `formatMoney` from the API string, never a float. Sums are in integer cents (`shared/lib/tier.ts` `cents` / `fromCents`).
- Touch targets are ≥ 44 px, form text is ≥ 16 px, and there is no horizontal scroll at 375.
- Color is never the only signal (D22): a tier color sits next to its label, and a rate dot next to its number.
- Copy is pt-BR as written here. Where Stitch's wording conflicts with shipped copy, shipped copy wins unless stated.

## 1. Próximos meses in reais (gap 2.1)

**Behavior.** Each month chip in `Próximos meses` (Mês) and in the Simular preview shows the month's **lowest projected balance** under the month name, in whole units of the user's currency, without the symbol. The tier label stays under the amount. The lowest balance, not the month-end, is shown because the chip's color is the lowest day's tier (D16). An end-of-month figure next to a red chip would contradict it.

- Source: `GET /api/forecast` (already loaded). Per month, the minimum `projected_balance` over the forecast days of that month, compared in cents. This is the same day set `monthsAhead` already uses for the tier.
- The current month's low covers today onward: the forecast starts today, so the month's past days do not count.
- Whole units: `floor(cents / 100)`, formatted with `Intl.NumberFormat("pt-BR", { maximumFractionDigits: 0 })` and the same minus sign as `formatMoney`. Flooring never shows more money than there is: −0,40 shows `-1`, and 1.842,99 shows `1.842`.
- The neutral (flat) state, `Lance seus próximos meses…`, keeps today's chips without amounts.

**Copy**

- Visible: `nov 2026` / `1.842` / `Confortável`.
- Accessible name: `nov 2026: Confortável, menor saldo R$ 1.842,10` (full money), with `, 3 dias no vermelho` appended when the month has red days.

**States**

- Loading and error: unchanged (`months-ahead.tsx:28-48`).
- Before the opening date: `antes do início`, no amount.
- Negative: `-12.345` in the tier's foreground.
- Five digits: `-12.345` and `12.345` fit a 3-column chip at 375 without clipping.

**Acceptance**

- A unit test of the per-month low: a negative low, the low on the last day, and a month with one forecast day.
- A unit test of the floor: `-0.40` → `-1`, `1842.99` → `1.842`, `0.00` → `0`.
- A chip test: the amount, the label, and the accessible name.
- e2e `months-ahead.spec.ts` at 375 with a five-digit low: no clipped node and `scrollWidth` ≤ 375.

## 8. Economia do ano (gap 6.1)

**Decision: built.** It is computed from existing transactions with one new report endpoint (D23).

- `Guardado` per month is exactly the month view's `savings.saved`: Economia bills (itself and cofrinhos) minus resgates, `months/repo.rs:46-63`.
- `Entradas` is the month view's `totals.income`: income without resgates.
- So every year row matches what Mês says for that month.

Nothing is stored, and there is no target or average (D16).

### 8.1 Endpoint

`GET /api/reports/savings?year=2026`, tag `reports`, session cookie.

```json
{
  "year": 2026,
  "income_total": "64482.80",
  "saved_total": "5416.53",
  "rate_pct": "8.4",
  "months": [
    { "month": "2026-11", "income": "6500.00", "saved": "776.13", "rate_pct": "11.9" },
    { "month": "2026-10", "income": "5921.20", "saved": "610.00", "rate_pct": "10.3" }
  ]
}
```

- Types in `crates/soldi-types/src/reports.rs`:
  - `SavingsYear { year: i32, income_total: Decimal, saved_total: Decimal, rate_pct: Option<Decimal>, months: Vec<SavingsMonth> }`
  - `SavingsMonth { month: String, income: Decimal, saved: Decimal, rate_pct: Option<Decimal> }`
  - Money goes through `crate::money`, and `rate_pct` is a string with one decimal or `null`, as `CategoryShare.delta_pct`.
- `months`: newest first. Every month from `min(December, today's month)` down to `max(January, the opening month)`. A month with no entries reads `"0.00"` / `"0.00"` / `null`.
- Bounds:
  - from `max(year-01-01, opening_date)`;
  - to `min(year-12-31, month_end(today))`, with today in São Paulo (D13);
  - future-dated entries inside today's month count, as on Mês;
  - months after today's are a forecast and are left out (D16).
- `rate_pct = saved / income × 100`, one decimal, half to even. This is `reports::domain::percent`, the function Mês uses. It is `null` when income is 0. Totals are summed over the listed months.
- A year outside the opening→today range returns `months: []`, totals `"0.00"`, and `rate_pct: null`.
- Errors (RFC 9457):
  - `400`: no `year`;
  - `422`: `year` is not an integer in 2000–2100 (SOL-30 bound);
  - `401`: no session.

**Query.** One statement, `repo::savings_by_month(conn, user_id, from, to)`:

```sql
SELECT date_trunc('month', t.date::timestamp)::date AS "month!",
       COALESCE(SUM(t.amount) FILTER (WHERE t.kind = 'income' AND t.cofrinho_id IS NULL), 0) AS "income!",
       COALESCE(SUM(CASE WHEN t.kind = 'bill' THEN t.amount ELSE -t.amount END)
                FILTER (WHERE (t.kind = 'bill' AND (c.is_savings OR COALESCE(p.is_savings, false)))
                           OR t.cofrinho_id IS NOT NULL), 0) AS "saved!"
FROM transactions t
JOIN categories c ON c.user_id = t.user_id AND c.id = t.category_id
LEFT JOIN categories p ON p.user_id = c.user_id AND p.id = c.parent_id
WHERE t.user_id = $1 AND t.date BETWEEN $2 AND $3
GROUP BY 1
```

- Settings are read once (`settings::repo::find`) for `opening_date`.
- The domain (`reports::domain::savings_year`) is pure: it fills the missing months, orders them, and computes the rates and totals.

**Isolation.** `t.user_id = $1` from `CurrentUser`, and both joins are pinned to the same `user_id`. There is no parameter that names a user.

**Performance budget**

- 2 queries: settings, plus the one above on `transactions_user_date_idx (user_id, date)`.
- At most 12 rows back, payload ≤ 2 KB.
- p95 ≤ 50 ms server time with 10 000 entries in the year.
- No N+1, no per-month loop against the database.

**Acceptance (backend)**

- Domain tests:
  - gap filling;
  - an opening date mid-year (the first row is the opening month, with only entries on or after the opening date);
  - the current year stopping at today's month;
  - income 0 → `null`;
  - negative saved (more resgates than savings);
  - half-to-even rounding;
  - totals.
- Integration `tests/reports.rs`:
  - each month row equals `GET /api/months/{m}` (`totals.income`, `savings.saved`, `savings.rate_pct`) for the same user;
  - user B's Economia entries never appear in A's year;
  - 400, 422 (`1999`, `abc`) and 401;
  - a year before the opening → `months: []`.
- `web/openapi.json` regenerated, `pnpm gen:api`, `scripts/sqlx-prepare.sh --check` clean.
