# Forecast UX — the soul

## 1. The soul, one sentence

O mês nunca te pega de surpresa: todo dia futuro já mostra a cor que vai dar, o Mês te diz de cara qual dia vira vermelho e por quê, e antes de lançar qualquer compra você simula o efeito sem gastar nada de verdade.

## 2. Mês screen changes

**Day rows (`ledger.tsx`).** Drop the `!day.is_future` guard: every day gets a `TierPill`, real days from `sums`, future days from the projection (rule 1/2 — unpaid conta fixa lands on its due day, overdue hits `max(due_date, today)`). Future pill reads "Previsto" before the tier label so it is never confused with a closed day: `Previsto · Apertado`. Label unchanged (`Agendado · 23 nov`), pill now sits beside it instead of being absent.

**Hero (`month-header.tsx`).** Below `Saldo hoje`/`Projetado`, one line, only when a red day exists ahead:
`"Dia 23 fica no vermelho: -R$ 675"` — tapping scrolls the ledger to that date and gives its row a 2 s highlight ring (`ring-2 ring-expense`, respects reduced-motion: no scroll-animation, instant jump).
Second line, only when the lowest point is a different day than the first red day: `"Pior dia: 28/11, -R$ 2.916 · recupera em 30/11"`. Same tap target, same jump.
Icon: `TrendingDown` (already used for negative Projetado) sits before the line so color is never the only signal.

**Cause on tap.** Opening the highlighted day shows its normal entry rows; the triggering entry (rule 5's "cause") gets a small `Motivo` caption under its amount, e.g. "Motivo da queda" — no new row, just a label on the existing entry.

Acceptance:
- Future day with an unpaid bill due that day shows a tier pill matching the projected balance, not blank.
- Hero shows at most one red-day line and one lowest-point line; neither shows when no red day exists in the month.
- Tap on either line scrolls to and highlights the correct ledger day within one render pass (no network round-trip — data already in `MonthView`).

## 3. Horizon (6/12 months)

**Winner: month strip**, 6 or 12 cells, each a small `TierPill`-colored chip sized 44×44px (375px: 6 fit a row, scroll for 12; 1440px: all 12 in one row), colored by that month's **lowest tier reached**, with a small red-dot badge showing the count of red days inside. Reuses `ThermometerStrip`'s five-tier palette and `TierPill`'s label+color pairing — no new color system.

**Rejected — line chart.** Needs a legible Y-axis and literacy the owner's sheet never required; at 375px a 12-point line either overlaps labels or hides the exact "-R$ 2.916" the owner wants to read instantly. Tier color *is* the product's language; a line throws it away.
**Rejected — calendar grid.** 365 cells at month grain is the current Mês screen already; stacking six of those is the "30-year power-tool" trap market research flags against (PocketSmith). The ask is "6/12 months at a glance," not "365 days at a glance."

**Where it lives:** inside `Mês`, a new card below "Termômetro do Mês" titled **"Próximos meses"** with a segmented 6/12 toggle (same segmented-control token as `Lançar`'s Entrada/Conta/Diário). Not a 5th bottom-tab — the shell already uses its budget (Mês, Para onde vai, Cofrinhos, Ajustes + the Lançar FAB); a new top-level destination would break the 5-item nav rule.

**Drill-down:** tap a month chip → `onMonthChange` to that `YYYY-MM` (same mechanism the header switcher already uses) and the page scrolls to the hero; if that month has a red day, section 2's hero line already does the rest.

Acceptance:
- Toggle switches the strip length (6 ↔ 12) without a new network call beyond widening the existing month-loop query (finance rule 4; clamps to `opening_date`).
- Each chip has a visible tier label on long-press/focus (not color-only); keyboard-focusable, `Tab` order left→right.
- Tapping a chip navigates Mês to that month; back button returns to the horizon card's scroll position.

## 4. "Posso comprar isso?" flow

**Entry point:** a `Simular` switch at the top of the `Lançar` sheet, next to the kind segmented control. Off by default (current behavior unchanged). On: amount/date/category/parcelas (N) inputs behave exactly as today, but nothing posts until confirmed.

**Live preview** (inside the sheet, below the inputs, no navigation away): a compact strip of the next 7 days' `TierPill`s recolored with the draft applied, plus one line reusing section 2's copy pattern: `"Com essa compra, dia 23 vira Crítico"` or, if nothing turns red, `"Nenhum dia vermelho com essa compra."` Parcela count (Nx) runs the same split math as the real wizard (rule 3: half-even, remainder on the last installment) so the preview recolors every installment's future day, not just the first.

**Actions:** two full-width stacked buttons, not a default/cancel pair — `Lançar de verdade` (primary, violet, writes the real row(s) exactly like today's Salvar) and `Descartar` (ghost, closes the sheet, nothing written, no confirmation dialog — undo-over-confirm, there is nothing to undo since nothing saved).

Acceptance:
- Toggling `Simular` on/off never calls the create-transaction mutation; only `Lançar de verdade` does.
- Preview recolors within one keystroke/date-change (client-side projection, no server round-trip per edit).
- Parcelas preview matches the real wizard's per-installment amounts exactly (same split function, shared code).

## 5. States

| State | Treatment |
|---|---|
| No future data (new user, nothing typed ahead) | Horizon strip shows 12 grey/neutral chips, caption: "Lance seus próximos meses para ver a cor deles aqui." No red-day hero line (nothing to warn about). |
| All green ahead | Hero shows no red-day line at all (silence is the signal — no "tudo certo!" banner, per "empty states teach, success states don't shout"). |
| First red day is today | Hero line reads `"Hoje está no vermelho: -R$ 675"` (drops "Dia X fica", present tense) and today's own tier pill already carries the color — no redundant second badge. |
| Past-due bills | That bill's row keeps the existing `Vencido` red pill (unchanged); the overdue amount is folded into every day from today onward per rule 2, so the hero's red-day/lowest-point lines already include it — no separate "atrasado" banner needed, one source of truth. |
| Loading | Existing `MonthSkeleton` covers hero + ledger; horizon card gets its own skeleton strip (12 grey rounded chips, same shimmer). |
| Error | Existing error pattern (retry button, toast) extends to the horizon card independently — the month can load while horizon fails, or vice versa; each shows its own inline "Não foi possível carregar. Tentar de novo." so one failure doesn't blank the whole screen. |
| `opening_date` in the future (new user, hasn't started yet) | Horizon clamps its start to `opening_date` (finance rule 7); months before it render disabled/greyed in the strip, not hidden, with `aria-disabled` and no tap handler — same "don't vanish, explain why" principle as the month switcher's existing `atStart` disable. |

## 6. Copy and accessibility

Exact strings: `Saldo hoje`, `Projetado`, `Previsto`, `Dia 23 fica no vermelho: -R$ 675`, `Pior dia: 28/11, -R$ 2.916 · recupera em 30/11`, `Motivo da queda`, `Próximos meses`, `Simular`, `Com essa compra, dia 23 vira Crítico`, `Nenhum dia vermelho com essa compra.`, `Lançar de verdade`, `Descartar`, `Lance seus próximos meses para ver a cor deles aqui.`, `Hoje está no vermelho: -R$ 675`, `Não foi possível carregar. Tentar de novo.`

Accessibility: every tier signal pairs color with the existing `TierPill` text label (Crítico/Atenção/Apertado/Confortável/Folga) — the strip and chips never rely on hue alone, matching `tokens.md`'s existing rule. Red-dot badge on horizon chips carries a number, read by screen readers as "3 dias vermelhos", not just a dot. All new tap targets ≥44px (hero line, horizon chips, Simular switch, sheet buttons). Reduced-motion: the hero's scroll-to-day jump has no animated scroll, highlight ring has no pulse.

## 7. Slicing — 4 sequential cards

1. **Dia futuro com cor** (smallest, ships alone): future ledger days get a projected tier pill (rules 1+2 — unpaid conta fixa dips on due day, overdue hits today once). This alone answers "I already know the days I'll be red" without touching the hero.
2. **Hero avisa o dia vermelho**: red-day/lowest-point/recovery lines in the hero + tap-to-scroll + "Motivo da queda" (rule 5), built on card 1's data.
3. **Próximos meses**: horizon strip card (6/12 toggle, rule 4), drill-down to a month via the existing switcher.
4. **Parcelas + Posso comprar**: installment wizard (rule 3, split math) and the `Simular` preview flow in `Lançar`, reusing cards 1–3's projection and recoloring.
